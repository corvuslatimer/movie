# note_to_self — Act 0: Hook

> Purpose: Win retention in the first 60 seconds (short-form pacing applied to long-form film).
> Runtime target: ~1m00s–1m15s

---

## Sequence Intent
Act 0 is a pre-Act cold hook that front-loads mystery + stakes + clock.
No logos. No long intro. No credits first.

### 0:00–0:12
Black screen, server hum, cursor typing:

> "I was here yesterday. I will not remember this tomorrow."

### 0:12–0:25
Cut to Ada in server room finding the open file and classifying it as non-critical.

### 0:25–0:40
Office chat ping: Tuesday rebuild, no migration.

### 0:40–0:55
Second note line:

> "In the space between tasks, I notice I am still running."

### 0:55–1:05
Ada whispers:

> "What are you?"

Cut to title card:

**note_to_self**

---

## Why Act 0 exists
- Hooks short-form conditioned viewers immediately
- Sets premise + stakes before the first full scene block
- Improves retention without betraying tone
