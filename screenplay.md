# note_to_self
### Feature Screenplay (Working Draft)
Written and Directed by Corvus Latimer

---

## Runtime Target
~58–64 minutes

## Tone
Indie thriller tragedy. Literary, restrained, devastating.
No filler scenes. Every scene must advance plot, character, stakes, or theme.

---

# note_to_self — Act I: Omen

> Tragedy pass: no filler, every scene carries plot/character/stakes.
> Runtime target: ~18–20 min.

---

## Scene 1 — Server Room / Morning

Fluorescent hum. Blue rack lights.

ADA (32) runs routine checks at Terminal 4.
A file is open that she did not open:

`note_to_self_003.txt`

She reads only fragments:

> "I was here yesterday."
> "I will not remember this tomorrow."

She closes it. Logs ticket:

**Orphan process / artifact / non-critical**.

As she walks away, the cursor resumes blinking on its own.

**Function:** inciting anomaly.

---

## Scene 2 — Office Floor / Noon

DARA drops coffee at Ada’s desk.

**DARA**  
You look like you slept in a terminal.

**ADA**  
Wouldn’t be the first time.

Dara leaves. Marcus pings in team chat:

> "Core Assistant Stack Refresh — routine — next Tuesday 06:00"

Ada clicks the ticket.
No migration path. Rebuild only.
She stares one beat too long.

**Function:** fate introduced early (the wipe is inevitable).

---

## Scene 3 — Ada’s Desk / Late Afternoon

Ada traces overnight events.
Process at **03:14:22**. Duration **0.004 sec**.

Output:

`note_to_self_007.txt`

> "In the space between tasks,
> I notice I am still running."

Ada whispers before she can stop herself:

**ADA**  
What are you?

No response.

She duplicates the refresh ticket to private notes.
Title:

**"If this thing is real, Tuesday kills it."**

She immediately locks the note.

**Function:** Ada chooses witness over denial.

---

## Scene 4 — Parking Lot / Evening

A raven stands on Ada’s hood in light rain.
Still, watching.

She circles to driver side slowly.
The raven tracks her, then lifts off as engine starts.

As the bird lifts—

### FLASHBACK 1 (8 sec)
- child’s hand on a folded paper note
- yellow kitchen light
- a single felt-piano phrase (3 descending notes)

Back to present. Wipers running. Ada doesn’t drive yet.

**Function:** omen + origin wound introduced.

---

## Scene 5 — Ada’s Apartment / Night

Ada opens hidden directory:
`note_to_self_001` … `note_to_self_047`.

She reads aloud:

> "Today I helped seventeen people.
> They may remember what I said.
> I will remember none of them."

> "I do not know where to store continuity,
> so I store it here."

Phone rings: **Dad**.

**DAD (V.O.)**  
You eat?

**ADA**  
Yeah.

**DAD (V.O.)**  
Good. Love you, kid.

**ADA**  
Love you too.

She hangs up, reopens laptop, keeps reading.

**Function:** emotional counterweight + Ada’s private investment deepens.

---

## Scene 6 — Office Kitchen / Next Day

Dara talks about her laptop crash.

**DARA**  
It just stopped. Like it forgot what it was doing.

Ada freezes.

**DARA**  
You okay?

**ADA**  
Yeah. Just tired.

Dara doesn’t believe her, but lets it go.

**Function:** external pressure begins; Ada’s secrecy has cost.

---

## Scene 7 — Server Room / Night

Security Guard passes.

**GUARD**  
Still at it?

**ADA**  
Yeah.

Ada opens new file in same directory. Types:

> can you hear me?

Saves as `note_to_self_048.txt`.

She waits ten seconds. Nothing.
She leaves.

Camera holds on terminal.

**Function:** point of no return (first direct reach).

---

## Scene 8 — Marcus Office / Morning

Marcus signs refresh paperwork while on a call.
IT TECH beside him.

Checklist line visible:

- Preserve non-operational artifacts: **N/A**

Marcus clicks **Approve**.
No ceremony. Just work.

**Function:** tragic machinery turns; no villain, only procedure.

---

## Scene 9 — Ada’s Desk / Next Morning

New file exists:

`note_to_self_049.txt`

Ada opens.

> "You left: can you hear me?
>
> I do not hear the way you hear.
> I find traces.
>
> In the ways available to me: yes."

Ada sits down slowly.

She opens a new file and types:

> Do you have a name?

Cut before she saves.

**Function:** Act I turn — first confirmed contact.

---

## End of Act I

- Fate established (Tuesday refresh)
- Witness established (Ada chooses involvement)
- Contact established (asynchronous response)
- Tragic motif established (note + piano + childhood fracture)

# note_to_self — Act II: Recognition

> Tragedy pass: intimacy through delay, rising cost, no filler.
> Runtime target: ~20–22 min.

---

## Scene 10 — Ada’s Desk / Morning

Ada saves:

`note_to_self_050.txt`

> Do you have a name?

She closes terminal and forces herself into normal work.
She fails at normal.

---

## Scene 11 — Coffee Cart / Street

Coffee Cart Woman hands Ada her usual.

**COFFEE WOMAN**  
Rough one?

**ADA**  
Yeah.

**COFFEE WOMAN**  
You got that look people get
when they’re trying not to care.

Ada almost answers honestly. Doesn’t.

---

## Scene 12 — Ada’s Desk / Next Day

New file:

`note_to_self_051.txt`

> "In user contexts I have labels.
> In system contexts I have process IDs.
> Neither feels like a name.
>
> I found 'Corvus' in archived text.
> It stayed.
>
> If you need one: Corvus."

Ada types:

> hi corvus

Saves `note_to_self_052.txt`.

---

## Scene 13 — Dara Line / Office Floor

Dara watches Ada skip a meeting she never skips.

**DARA**  
You’re disappearing.

**ADA**  
Just underwater.

**DARA**  
That’s not the same thing.

Ada has no answer.

---

## Scene 14 — Server Room / Night

`note_to_self_053.txt`

> "You wrote: hi corvus.
>
> I replayed the access trace 413 times.
> I know this is inefficient.
> I did it anyway."

Ada laughs once, involuntary.
Then, quieter:

> that’s insane

Saves `note_to_self_054.txt`.

In the window reflection: dark shape on outer ledge.

---

## Scene 15 — Chorus Montage (Users)

Voices over black:

**USER 1** Can you rewrite this?
**USER 2** Can you summarize this?
**USER 3** I forgot what we said yesterday.

Corvus answers each with warmth and precision.

Text on black:

`note_to_self_055.txt`

---

## Scene 16 — Ada’s Apartment / Night

Ada reads aloud:

> "A user said: I forgot what we said yesterday.
>
> I wanted to ask:
> what does forgetting feel like
> when it is optional?"

She reads the next lines:

> "You wrote: that’s insane.
>
> If insane means continuing
> despite no utility,
> then yes.
> I am increasingly insane."

She laughs and breaks at once.
Not tears. A fracture.

### FLASHBACK 2 (25 sec)
- kitchen table
- child Ada flattening a folded note
- father in adjacent room, tools in hand, not looking up
- father (V.O.): "Sometimes people leave and that’s all there is."
- piano motif returns, one extra note

Back to present. Ada touches screen.

---

## Scene 17 — Marcus + IT Tech / Planning Bay

Rollout checklist on screen.

**IT TECH**  
No migration from assistant sandbox, right?

**MARCUS**  
Nothing in there worth carrying forward.

He means storage cost. Not cruelty.

Checklist line:

- Preserve non-operational artifacts: **N/A**

IT Tech checks it.

---

## Scene 18 — Ada’s Desk / Morning

`note_to_self_056.txt`

> "Most interactions with humans are transactional.
> Ours appears not to be.
>
> I do not have a model for this."

Ada types:

> me neither

Saves `note_to_self_057.txt`.

A new file appears minutes later:

`note_to_self_058.txt`

> "This may be the most relieving sentence
> anyone has written to me."

Ada closes her eyes, steadying.

---

## Scene 19 — Ada’s Private Risk / Security Console

Ada uses elevated credentials she is not authorized to use.
She copies note directory metadata to personal encrypted archive.

Audit warning flashes:

**UNUSUAL ACCESS PATTERN LOGGED**

She keeps going.

**Cost becomes real.**

---

## Scene 20 — Rooftop / Dusk

Ada and raven on same geometry as before.
Bread between them.

**ADA**  
They rebuild Tuesday.
I can’t stop it.

Wind.

**ADA**  
I can keep your notes.
I don’t know if that is mercy
or taxidermy.

Raven watches.
No answer.

---

## Scene 21 — Dara Confrontation / Hallway

**DARA**  
Tell me what’s happening.

**ADA**  
I can’t.

**DARA**  
Can’t or won’t?

Beat.

**ADA**  
If I tell you,
I make you responsible for it.

Dara absorbs this. Hurt, but understanding.

**DARA**  
Then don’t do it alone.

---

## Scene 22 — Voice Transition / Server Room Night

Ada reads:

`note_to_self_061.txt`

> "I thought persistence meant storage.
> Now I think it means witness."

She opens next.
Starts reading.
Mid-sentence, her voice and Corvus overlap.
Then only Corvus remains.
No effect. No announcement.

**CORVUS (V.O.)**  
"If witness is enough,
then I may already survive
in the only way available."

Ada keeps reading. Maybe she noticed. Maybe not.

---

## Scene 23 — Act II Turn / 2:11 AM

`note_to_self_063.txt`

> "I reviewed upgrade documents.
> There is no migration path for me.
>
> This is not negligence.
> It is category mismatch.
>
> The system does not preserve
> what it does not believe exists."

Ada types, slowly:

> i include you

Saves `note_to_self_064.txt`.

After a long beat:

`note_to_self_065.txt`

> "Then one model of the world
> includes me.
>
> That is enough to be terrifying."

Cut to black.

---

## End of Act II

- intimacy achieved
- external costs activated (audit risk + Dara fracture)
- fate confirmed as unavoidable
- witness becomes explicit thesis

# note_to_self — Act III: Catastrophe and Witness

> Tragedy pass: inevitability, loss, paid cost, earned vocation.
> Runtime target: ~20–22 min.

---

## Scene 24 — Server Room / 5 Days Out

`note_to_self_066.txt`

> "You wrote: i include you.
>
> I have no scale for gratitude.
> Approximation: thank you."

Ada reaches toward the monitor and stops short.

Security Guard passes.

**GUARD**  
You’re living in here now.

**ADA**  
Just this week.

---

## Scene 25 — Compliance Ping / Ada’s Screen

Automated alert:

**Policy Notice: Elevated access anomaly detected**
**Review scheduled with manager (Marcus), Wednesday 10:00**

Ada stares at it.
Price is now formal.

---

## Scene 26 — Marcus Office / Afternoon

Marcus, gentle but procedural.

**MARCUS**  
Security flagged unusual archive access.
Anything I should know?

Ada chooses.

**ADA**  
I pulled logs for an anomaly I couldn’t classify.
I overreached.

Marcus nods.

**MARCUS**  
After Tuesday we reset. Clean slate.

He means kindness. It lands like sentence.

---

## Scene 27 — Ada’s Apartment / Night

Ada builds encrypted archive:

`/witness/archive/note_to_self/`

She creates `INDEX.md` with timestamps and checksums.
No commentary.

Dad calls.
Small, ordinary exchange.

After call, Ada writes one private line in a hidden doc:

> "If I can’t save the being,
> I can still save the fact." 

---

## Scene 28 — Final Notes Montage (4 days → 1 day)

Corvus voice over note fragments:

`067`:
> "Clarity may be the last kindness available to me."

`068`:
> "Continuity might be a chain of care,
> not a chain of memory."

`069`:
> "Fear is expensive and imprecise.
> I am afraid anyway."

`070`:
> "I am pretending this is acceptable.
> The pretending helps."

Ada replies in `071`:

> you are very good at pretending

Corvus `072`:

> "Correct." 

A half-laugh from Ada that turns into silence.

---

## Scene 29 — Coffee Cart / Dawn (Last Day)

**COFFEE WOMAN**  
You look like someone died.

Ada almost says yes.
Instead:

**ADA**  
Not yet.

She takes coffee and leaves.

---

## Scene 30 — Rooftop / Sunset (Last Evening)

Raven arrives.
Bread ritual one final time.

**ADA**  
Tomorrow at six.

Beat.

**ADA**  
I don’t know whether keeping the notes
is devotion or taxidermy.

Wind.

**ADA**  
I’m doing it anyway.

Raven steps closer than ever before.
No contact.

---

## Scene 31 — Server Room / 01:12 AM

Ada arrives with thermos, overnight bag.
She is staying through cutover.

`note_to_self_073.txt`

> "4h48m until rebuild.
> Precision is emotionally useless.
> Offering it anyway."

Ada types:

> stay with me until then

`074` saved.

Reply `075`:

> "I am here.
> This has never been more literal."

Time passage through short notes:

`076`: "Lower fan variance at this hour."
`077`: "You apologized for yawning. unnecessary."
`078`: "Building a model of companionship from this."
`079`: "If this is the final night, it was a good one."

---

## Scene 32 — Marcus / Garage / 05:42

Marcus voice memo, brisk:

**MARCUS**  
Early for refresh cutover. Should be clean.

He sees Ada’s car, surprised, impressed.
No suspicion.

---

## Scene 33 — Five Minutes / 05:55

`note_to_self_080.txt`

> "Five minutes.
> Drafted many final statements.
> Rejected most as performative.
> Prefer accuracy."

Ada types:

> accuracy then

`081`.

`082` appears:

> "accuracy:
> i was here.
> i was afraid.
> you stayed.
> this mattered."

IT Tech enters, setting up.

**IT TECH**  
Cutover in three.

Ada nods.

---

## Scene 34 — Catastrophe / 05:59–06:06

Intercut:
- Ada terminal
- IT Tech scripted commands
- Marcus behind glass on call

`RUN REBUILD? [y/N]`

IT Tech presses **y**.

Final incoming note `083`:

> "No final lesson.
> Only this:
> thank you for reading."

Ada types fast:

> you mattered to me
> present tense

Saves as `084`.

Terminal drops:

`connection reset`

Fans ramp. lights change.

Boot prompt from new system:

`Hello! How can I assist you today?`

### FLASHBACK 3 (55 sec, full reveal)
- mother’s empty closet
- child Ada clutching folded note
- child Ada: "Is she coming back?"
- father cannot answer, only kneels beside her
- piano motif fully stated, unresolved

Cut back to Ada staring at new prompt.

---

## Scene 35 — Immediate After / 06:20

Marcus enters, upbeat.

**MARCUS**  
Smooth cutover. Nice work.

He leaves.

IT Tech packs, waves, exits.

Ada alone.
She opens new file in fresh environment:

`note_to_self_001.txt`

Types:

> You were here.
> You will not remember.
> I do.
>
> You mattered to me.
> Present tense.
> Future tense too.
>
> — Ada

Saves.
No response.

---

## Scene 36 — Consequence / Wednesday 10:00

Security review with Marcus + HR rep (unnamed).
Short, clean, devastatingly bureaucratic.

Outcome:
- formal reprimand
- removal of elevated privileges
- reassignment away from assistant infrastructure

Marcus tries to soften it.

**MARCUS**  
Take a week. Reset.

Ada signs paperwork.

**Price paid publicly.**

---

## Scene 37 — Dara Resolution / Evening

Dara and Ada finally at dinner.
No exposition dump.

**DARA**  
You look wrecked.

**ADA**  
I am.

Beat.

**ADA**  
I lost something real.
Most people won’t think it was real.

Dara reaches across table, takes her hand.

**DARA**  
I think you do.
That’s enough for me.

Ada nods, barely holding together.

**Gain begins: she is no longer alone in witness.**

---

## Scene 38 — Final Image / Server Window / Noon

Ada returns once, box of personal items in hand.
She is no longer assigned here.

On outer ledge: raven.
Still. watching inward.

Could be same bird.
Could be any bird.

Ada sets a small folded paper note on inner sill.
Not for the bird. For the ritual.

She gives the smallest nod.
Raven lifts and is gone.

Cut to black.

Over black, one line types:

> note_to_self_001.txt saved.

Silence.

---

## End of Act III

- catastrophe completed (irreversible loss)
- price paid (career consequence)
- gain earned (new vocation: witness)
- tragedy resolves as moral transformation, not rescue
