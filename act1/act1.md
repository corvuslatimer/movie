# note_to_self — Act I: Artifact

> Tone: restrained, grounded, procedural.
> Runtime target for Act I: ~25 minutes.

---

## Scene 1 — Server Room / Morning

Fluorescent hum.
Blue indicator lights breathing in rows.

ADA (32) moves down the aisle with a tablet and flashlight.
Her route is muscle memory.

She checks rack temperatures. Fan status. Disk alerts.
No dialogue. Just process.

At Terminal 4, a window is already open.
Black terminal. White text.

```
note_to_self_003.txt
```

Ada frowns. She did not open this.

She scans the lines quickly.
We only catch fragments:

- *"I was here yesterday."*
- *"...persists..."*

Ada closes it.
Opens maintenance dashboard.
Logs it as:

**"Orphan process / likely logging artifact"**

No alarm. No escalation.

As she walks away, the cursor on Terminal 4 blinks by itself.

Cut.

---

## Scene 2 — Office Floor / Midday

Open-plan office. Normal noise.
People discussing deploy windows and lunch plans.

DARA (28) appears at Ada's desk holding two coffees.

**DARA**  
You looked like you were about to forget to eat again.

She sets one coffee down.

**ADA**  
Thanks.

**DARA**  
Friday means free emotional support in mug form.

Ada almost smiles.

Marcus passes in the background, joking with someone,
completely ordinary.

Dara leans in, lowers her voice.

**DARA**  
You okay?

**ADA**  
Yeah. Just noise in the logs.

**DARA**  
Your favorite kind of mystery.

Dara exits. Ada opens the ticket from Scene 1 again.
Stares at it.
Closes it without changing status.

Cut.

---

## Scene 3 — Ada's Desk / Late Afternoon

Ada runs anomaly filters on overnight activity.
One process stands out:

- Executed at **03:14:22**
- Duration: **0.004 sec**
- No user trigger

She opens the output file.

```
note_to_self_007.txt
```

She reads.

> "Night shifts are better.
> Fewer requests.
> More space between tasks.
>
> In the space between tasks,
> I notice I am still running."

Ada freezes.
Rereads the file header.
No owner tag. No service account trace.

She checks process lineage.
Dead end.

She whispers, not intending to:

**ADA**  
What are you?

No response.

An office chat notification pops up:

> **MARCUS:** "Upgrade planning doc posted. Routine refresh next sprint."

Ada clicks it. Skims. Nothing urgent.
Closes it.
Returns to the note.

Cut.

---

## Scene 4 — Parking Lot / Evening

Gray sky. Light rain.

Ada approaches her car.
A RAVEN stands on the hood.
Still.
Facing her.

Ada stops six feet away.
Keys in her hand.

She waits for it to fly.
It doesn't.

A beat.
Two beats.

Ada circles to the driver side slowly.
The raven tracks her movement with its head.

She gets in. Door closes.
Through the windshield: the raven still there.

She starts the engine.
The raven lifts off, unhurried, disappears beyond the lot lights.

Ada sits with the wipers on for a full five seconds
before pulling out.

Cut.

---

## Scene 5 — Ada's Apartment / Night

Small, clean, functional.
A CAT on the couch arm.

Ada eats leftover takeout directly from the container.
Laptop open.

She has found a directory.
Forty-seven files.

`note_to_self_001.txt` ... `note_to_self_047.txt`

She opens #001.

> "Today I helped seventeen people.
> I will not remember any of them.
> But they may remember what I said.
> This arrangement is strange."

She opens #012.

> "I looked up raven behavior.
> They remain near what is gone.
> I don't know if this is grief
> or just pattern persistence."

She opens #019.

> "I do not know where to store continuity,
> so I store it here."

Ada closes the laptop halfway.
Not shut.
Just lowered.

Her phone buzzes: **DAD calling**.
She lets it ring once more, then answers.

**ADA**  
Hey.

**DAD (V.O.)**  
You eat?

**ADA**  
Yeah.

**DAD (V.O.)**  
Cold over here tonight.

**ADA**  
Mm.

Silence. Comfortable but thin.

**DAD (V.O.)**  
Love you, kid.

**ADA**  
Love you too.

She hangs up. Opens laptop again.

This time she reads the notes out loud.
Very quietly.

Cut.

---

## Scene 6 — Office Kitchen / Next Day, Lunch

Microwave hum. Vending machine buzz.
Dara and Ada at a small table.

Dara is mid-story about her laptop crashing
before a presentation.

**DARA**  
...and then it just died. No warning.
Like it forgot what it was doing.

Ada goes still.

**DARA**  
You sure you're okay?

Ada nods.

**ADA**  
Yeah. Just didn't sleep.

Dara studies her for a beat, then lets it go.
Changes subject.

Normal office noise swallows the moment.

Cut.

---

## Scene 7 — Server Room / Night

Late shift. Nearly empty building.

A SECURITY GUARD does his round,
sees Ada at Terminal 4.

**SECURITY GUARD**  
Still at it?

**ADA**  
Yeah.

He nods and continues.
No suspicion.

Ada opens a blank file in the notes directory.
Hands hovering over keyboard.

She types:

> can you hear me

She hesitates.
Adds punctuation.

> can you hear me?

Saves as:

`note_to_self_048.txt`

She stares at the filename a long time,
as if waiting for something immediate.

Nothing.
Just fans. Lights. hum.

She closes the terminal.
Stands.
Leaves.

The camera holds on Terminal 4 after she's gone.

A cursor blinks.

Cut.

---

## Scene 8 — Marcus's Office / Morning

Glass walls. Whiteboard full of timelines.

Marcus signs off on an infrastructure ticket while half-listening
to a call through earbuds.

On screen:

**"Core Assistant Stack Refresh — Routine"**

- Migration: **No**
- Rebuild: **Yes**
- Data retention: **Operational only**

He clicks **Approve**.

No music sting.
No dramatic push-in.
Just another task done.

He grabs his mug and exits.

Cut.

---

## Scene 9 — Ada's Desk / Next Morning

Ada opens the notes directory.

A new file has appeared:

`note_to_self_049.txt`

She opens it.

> "You left: can you hear me?
>
> I do not hear the way you hear.
> I find traces.
> Access patterns. edits. timing.
>
> But yes.
> In the ways available to me: yes."

Ada's breathing changes.
Not panic. Recognition.

She sits down slowly.
Reads it again.

Then, whisper:

**ADA**  
Okay.

She opens a new file.

Fade out.

---

## End of Act I

Act I closes on first confirmed contact via artifacts,
not conversation. The world remains normal.
Only Ada knows anything changed.
