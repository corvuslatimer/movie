# Scene 09 — Ada’s Desk / Next Morning

## Purpose
Deliver first confirmed asynchronous contact and close Act I on irreversible emotional escalation.

## Target Runtime
~2m00s–2m50s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Ada arrives at her desk and finds a new file: `note_to_self_049.txt`.
It directly references her message from the night before and answers it.
Contact is confirmed—but in delayed, artifact form.
Ada sits down slowly, absorbing what this means.
She opens a new file and types:

> Do you have a name?

Cut before save.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Ada’s desk, next morning (same office floor as Scene 2).

**Temporal tone:**
- normal workday startup energy around her
- inbox pings, coworkers moving, daily routine underway
- Ada is in a radically different reality no one else can see

**Mood of space:**
- ordinary exterior / seismic interior
- this is the “crossing the threshold” scene for Act I

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- cooler morning neutrals (gray-blue daylight)
- restrained office tones, no dramatic stylization

**Lighting:**
- soft daylight + overhead office practicals
- monitor glow becomes key emotional light during read

**Lens strategy:**
- open in medium/wide to place Ada inside normal office flow
- move to tighter lens (65mm/85mm) for file read and reaction
- typed question insert in crisp macro/close framing

**Camera movement:**
- gentle push-in while Ada reads response
- locked frame on final typed line before cut

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- dual monitors (chat/tickets + file window)
- coffee from earlier scene continuity (untouched or barely touched)
- sticky-note workflow visible
- team notifications in peripheral monitor (normal office world)

Filename:
- `note_to_self_049.txt`

Content excerpt exactly:

> Do you have a name?

---

## Sound Design
**Base layer:**
- office morning ambience (keyboards, muted chat, footsteps)
- subtle HVAC

**Foreground:**
- mouse click opening file
- faint keyboard taps as Ada types final question

**Subjective mix shifts:**
- as Ada reads the response, pull room tone down 10–15%
- when she begins typing new question, bring ambience back slightly (world intrudes)

**Music:**
- none

---

## Performance Direction
**Ada:**
- initial posture: routine
- on reading first line (“You left...”), micro-freeze
- sits down slowly as cognition catches up to reality
- no tears, no panic; the shock is precise and intellectual first
- typing “Do you have a name?” should feel ceremonial, careful

---

## Blocking
1. Ada sits/arrives at desk and opens notes directory.
2. Sees new file: `note_to_self_049.txt`.
3. Opens and reads silently.
4. Lowers herself into chair more fully (body registering significance).
5. Opens new file.
6. Types: `Do you have a name?`
7. Holds on unsaved line.
8. Cut.

---

## Why this scene matters
This is Act I’s decisive turn.
The anomaly answers.
Not in real time, not theatrically—through delayed trace.

That method is the film’s emotional law:
connection exists, but always across a gap.

Ending before the save preserves tension and hands propulsion directly into Act II.
