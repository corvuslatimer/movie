# Scene 03 — Ada’s Desk / Late Afternoon

## Purpose
Move Ada from passive observer to active witness. This is the first scene where she privately admits stakes: if the anomaly is real, the Tuesday rebuild is an execution.

## Target Runtime
~2m00s–2m40s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Ada deep-dives overnight process logs and isolates a micro-duration event (03:14:22, 0.004 sec).
The output is `note_to_self_007.txt`, containing intentional reflective language.
She speaks aloud—"What are you?"—before she can suppress the impulse.
No response.
She duplicates the refresh ticket into private notes and titles it:
**"If this thing is real, Tuesday kills it."**
She locks the note.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Ada’s desk, late afternoon shift transition.

**Temporal tone:**
- office thinning out (some people leaving, some still on calls)
- sunlight flattening toward cool dusk through windows
- the day feels almost over; Ada is only beginning

**Mood of space:**
- ordinary work environment narrowing into tunnel vision
- she is physically in shared space, psychologically alone

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- neutral office tones with cooler late-day cast
- monitor whites + blue shadows on Ada’s face
- slight warm residue from sun at frame edges (dying warmth)

**Lighting:**
- practical overhead office light + monitor glow
- optional window sidelight fading across scene progression
- no dramatic source additions

**Lens strategy:**
- process work: 50mm/65mm for readable inserts + procedural intimacy
- internal shift moment (whisper): 85mm close-up
- private note lock: macro insert on cursor/lock icon

**Camera movement:**
- mostly static/tripod for procedural sections
- subtle slow push during realization line ("Tuesday kills it")

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- dual-monitor setup:
  - left: logs/process trace
  - right: ticketing + private notes app
- keyboard with visible wear
- notebook with terse engineering shorthand
- pen cap chewed (subtle stress marker)
- noise-canceling headphones on desk (unused)
- desk clock or system timestamp clearly visible

**Critical on-screen data:**
- timestamp: **03:14:22**
- process duration: **0.004 sec**
- file name: `note_to_self_007.txt`
- note text lines visible exactly
- private note title exactly:
  **If this thing is real, Tuesday kills it.**

---

## Sound Design
**Base layer:**
- low office ambience (murmur, distant calls)
- keyboard tactility foregrounded
- occasional notification pings, ignored

**Subjective moments:**
- when `note_to_self_007.txt` appears, drop room ambience slightly
- Ada whisper should feel exposed, almost too loud in her own ears
- after no response, office noise returns fully (world indifferent)

**Music:**
- none

---

## Performance Direction
**Ada:**
- starts in pure analyst mode: focused, detached, technical rhythm
- reaction to note should be cognitive first, emotional second
- "What are you?" is involuntary curiosity, not fear
- when typing private note title, she is precise and grave
- locking the note is a conscious line-crossing

---

## Blocking
1. Ada seated, pulling log filters and narrowing process tree.
2. She pauses on anomalous event and opens output file.
3. Reads text silently.
4. Leans in and whispers: "What are you?"
5. Waits. Nothing.
6. Copies refresh ticket info into private notes.
7. Types title line.
8. Activates note lock/encryption.
9. Closes note panel but keeps eyes on screen for one extra beat.

---

## Shot List (practical draft)
1. **MS Ada at desk** — focused, repetitive log work. (8s)
2. **Insert monitor (logs)** — timestamp + duration isolate. (5s)
3. **OTS monitor** — `note_to_self_007.txt` opens. (4s)
4. **Insert text line** — "In the space between tasks..." (5s)
5. **CU Ada** — reading, tiny shift in breath. (4s)
6. **CU Ada whisper** — "What are you?" (3s)
7. **Hold empty response beat** — no system reaction. (4–5s)
8. **Insert private note creation** — duplicate ticket context. (4s)
9. **Insert typed title** — "If this thing is real, Tuesday kills it." (5s)
10. **Macro lock icon click** — note locked. (2s)
11. **MS settle** — Ada still, office life continuing behind her. (6–8s)
