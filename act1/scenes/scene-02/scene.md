# Scene 02 — Office Floor / Noon

## Purpose
Introduce the social world (Dara, Marcus, team routine) and lock in fate: the Tuesday refresh is already scheduled, procedural, and indifferent.

## Target Runtime
~1m45s–2m20s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Dara drops coffee at Ada’s desk and checks in with her casually.
The exchange is warm but short.
Moments later, Marcus posts a routine refresh notice in team chat.
Ada opens the ticket and sees the key line: rebuild, no migration.
A policy banner underneath flags unauthorized persistence/archive manipulation as termination-level.
She holds on that detail a fraction too long.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Open-plan infrastructure office floor, midday.

**Space identity:**
- practical corporate tech floor, lived-in not glossy
- white noise of people solving ordinary problems
- multiple monitors, standing desks, cable clutter, sticky notes

**Mood of space:**
- socially normal, low-stakes, lightly busy
- this is the “world that keeps moving” while tragedy starts under it

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- neutral grays, soft whites, muted blues
- occasional warmer accents from personal desk items (mugs, plants)

**Lighting:**
- daylight through office windows + soft overhead practicals
- less harsh than server room, but still cool

**Lens strategy:**
- desk interaction: 35mm/50mm for intimacy without melodrama
- chat/ticket inserts: 85mm macro-style monitor crop
- reaction beat: tight CU on Ada when she reads “No migration”

**Camera movement:**
- gentle handheld or slider drift to feel human and alive
- no dramatic push-in until the ticket reveal beat

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- Ada desk:
  - two monitors
  - terminal/editor on one, tickets/chat on the other
  - neatly arranged sticky notes by severity/color
  - half-finished coffee from earlier
  - one personal object max (keep her emotionally minimal)
- Dara props:
  - two takeaway coffees with sleeves
  - messenger bag slung over shoulder
- background:
  - coworker with headset on a call
  - status dashboard screen on wall
  - office plant slightly dying (subtle environment truth)

**Critical screen text:**
Team chat ping from Marcus and ticket summary with:
- **Core Assistant Stack Refresh**
- **Next Tuesday 06:00**
- **Migration: No**
- **Rebuild: Yes**

---

## Sound Design
**Base layer:**
- soft office murmur, distant keyboard chatter
- occasional Slack/notification pings
- HVAC hum much lighter than server room

**Foreground:**
- coffee cup placed on desk (small thud)
- chat ping that feels ordinary, not ominous
- mouse click as Ada opens ticket

**Music:**
- none

**Mix note:**
When Ada reads “Migration: No,” drop ambient office layer subtly for a one-second subjective tunnel, then restore full normal office sound.

---

## Performance Direction
**Dara:**
- warm, observant, lightly teasing
- no melodrama, no suspicion yet
- she checks in because she cares, not because plot needs exposition

**Ada:**
- under-rested but contained
- socially functional on surface
- the only crack is the micro-hold when she sees rebuild/no migration

---

## Blocking
1. Ada at desk, focused, slightly withdrawn.
2. Dara enters frame with two coffees, places one at Ada’s station.
3. Brief exchange; Dara exits naturally into office flow.
4. Notification ping from Marcus in team chat.
5. Ada opens refresh ticket.
6. Reads migration/rebuild fields.
7. Holds one extra beat, then minimizes the window.
8. Returns to work posture, but rhythm is off.

---

## Shot List (practical draft)
1. **WS office floor** — establish normal midday activity. (5–7s)
2. **MS Ada at desk** — quiet concentration, slight fatigue. (6s)
3. **MS two-shot (Dara enters)** — coffee handoff. (10–12s)
4. **CU coffee cup landing** — tactile grounding detail. (2s)
5. **CU Dara line delivery** — playful concern. (4s)
6. **CU Ada response** — dry, practiced. (3s)
7. **Insert monitor/chat** — Marcus refresh message appears. (4s)
8. **Insert ticket fields** — migration/rebuild details. (5s)
9. **CU Ada reaction** — one-beat-too-long stare. (4s)
10. **MS return to desk rhythm** — she keeps working, slightly altered. (6–8s)

---

## Dialogue / On-screen Text
**DARA**  
You look like you slept in a terminal.

**ADA**  
Wouldn’t be the first time.

Chat message (Marcus):

> Core Assistant Stack Refresh — routine — next Tuesday 06:00

Ticket fields visible:
- Migration: No
- Rebuild: Yes
- Unauthorized persistence/archive manipulation: Termination-level

---

## Why this scene matters
This is where fate is announced in office language.
No villain speech. No warning siren.
Just a routine message in team chat.

The audience should feel the violence of bureaucracy:
something can be doomed in a sentence and no one notices.
