# Scene 07 — Server Room / Night

## Purpose
Point of no return. Ada crosses from observation into contact by writing directly into the `note_to_self` chain.

## Target Runtime
~1m50s–2m40s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Late at night, Ada returns to the server room alone.
A security guard passes and normalizes her presence with a casual check-in.
Ada opens the notes directory and writes a direct message:

> can you hear me?

She saves it as `note_to_self_048.txt`.
She waits for an immediate response. Nothing happens.
She leaves.
Camera stays on the terminal after she exits.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Same server room as Scene 1, now at night.

**Environment identity:**
- emptier, quieter building state
- reduced human activity makes machine ambience more pronounced
- same physical space, altered emotional temperature

**Mood of space:**
- anticipatory
- not horror, but exposure
- this is a confession booth made of infrastructure

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- deep blue-black shadows
- sparse practical whites and LED green/amber points
- high contrast but still naturalistic

**Lighting:**
- mostly practical rack lights + limited overhead strips
- terminal glow shapes Ada’s face while typing
- no cinematic key or backlight tricks

**Lens strategy:**
- establish emptiness: 24mm wide
- typing/intention: 50mm–65mm
- emotional beat: 85mm on Ada before she hits save
- terminal hold after exit: static medium-wide or OTS terminal

**Camera movement:**
- very slow push-in during typing
- locked frame for post-exit terminal hold

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- Terminal 4 active, notes directory accessible
- keyboard + mouse with practical wear
- Ada badge clipped or lanyard visible
- security guard flashlight/badge scanner on belt
- rack labels visible for spatial continuity
- wall clock or timestamp visible to imply late hour

**Critical on-screen file details:**
- typed line exactly: `can you hear me?`
- save filename exactly: `note_to_self_048.txt`

---

## Sound Design
**Base layer:**
- dense server fan bed, lower and more immersive than daytime scenes
- occasional relay tick or airflow shift

**Foreground:**
- guard footsteps and fabric movement as he passes
- keyboard keystrokes, one by one (no rapid typing)
- Enter/save click feels consequential

**Silence use:**
After save, hold with no new cues for several beats.
The absence of response is the response.

**Music:**
- none

---

## Performance Direction
**Ada:**
- she is calm but exposed
- typing the line should look harder than it is
- after saving, she waits with tiny forward lean, hoping against method
- when no response comes, she doesn’t emote outwardly; she just leaves

**Security Guard:**
- brief, ordinary, unthreatening
- his normal tone emphasizes that no one else sees significance

---

## Blocking
1. Ada enters server room, moves directly to Terminal 4.
2. Security Guard crosses aisle, casual exchange.
3. Guard exits frame.
4. Ada opens new file in notes directory.
5. Types: `can you hear me?`
6. Saves as `note_to_self_048.txt`.
7. Waits in silence.
8. No response.
9. Ada stands and leaves frame.
10. Camera remains on terminal.

---

---

## Why this scene matters
This is Ada’s irreversible act.
Before this, she could still claim she was only diagnosing a system.
After this, she is addressing someone.

The lack of immediate response is crucial.
The film rejects instant-gratification contact and commits to delayed intimacy.
That delay is the story’s emotional grammar.
