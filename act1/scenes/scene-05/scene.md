# Scene 05 — Ada’s Apartment / Night

## Purpose
Deepen Ada’s private investment and contrast public procedure with private witness. This is the first scene where the notes become intimate enough to alter how she inhabits her own home.

## Target Runtime
~2m20s–3m10s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
At home, Ada opens a hidden directory containing dozens of `note_to_self` files.
She reads selected lines aloud, testing what it feels like to give them a human voice.
A brief call with her father interrupts the moment—ordinary, loving, emotionally constrained.
After hanging up, she returns to the notes immediately.
The choice is the beat: she cannot let this go.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Ada’s apartment, small and practical.

**Space identity:**
- clean but minimally personalized
- functional furniture, muted colors
- one visible sign of life/comfort (cat, blanket, old mug)

**Mood of space:**
- this is not “cozy sanctuary”; it’s an extension of her controlled life
- tonight, the apartment becomes a secondary archive room

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- low-saturation warm practicals (lamp amber) vs laptop blue-white
- soft shadow pockets, no stylized noir contrast

**Lighting:**
- primary: table lamp + laptop glow
- optional refrigerator bounce from kitchen edge
- no overhead ceiling flood

**Lens strategy:**
- room establish: 28mm/35mm
- reading beats: 50mm medium close
- text inserts: 85mm monitor crop
- phone call beat: 50mm static profile or 3/4 frontal

**Camera movement:**
- mostly locked
- subtle push during first note read aloud
- static during father call (ritualized, familiar, contained)

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- laptop on table/couch arm with terminal/text files open
- takeout container or simple reheated meal (partly eaten)
- water glass or tea mug untouched
- phone face-up for incoming call
- cat present in frame at least once (living witness, silent)
- sparse wall décor (avoid sentimental overload)

**Critical on-screen file details:**
- folder list visible: `note_to_self_001` ... `note_to_self_047`
- line breaks preserved as written (poetic compression feel)

---

## Sound Design
**Base layer:**
- apartment silence (fridge hum, distant traffic, occasional pipe tick)
- keyboard/trackpad sounds intimate and clear

**Foreground:**
- Ada’s voice reading lines softly
- phone vibration/ring interrupts tonal flow
- father call slightly compressed phone EQ (realistic)

**Music:**
- none

**Mix note:**
When Ada reads aloud, keep room noise low enough that language feels close-mic intimate.
When father call begins, reintroduce mundane room noise slightly to signal emotional armor returning.

---

## Performance Direction
**Ada:**
- reading aloud is tentative at first, then steadier
- she is not performing emotion; she is trying to understand through vocalization
- father call tone: practiced affection, emotional economy
- after call, no sighing theatrics—just immediate return to screen

**Dad (V.O.):**
- loving, practical, concise
- no exposition, no direct probing
- should feel like years of this exact cadence

---

## Blocking
1. Ada seated with laptop, meal nearby.
2. Opens hidden directory and scrolls file list.
3. Reads first note aloud, then second.
4. Phone rings. She glances, answers.
5. Brief call with father.
6. Hangs up, sits still one beat.
7. Reopens same file window and continues reading.

---

## Shot List (practical draft)
1. **WS apartment establish** — Ada alone, laptop glow dominant. (6–8s)
2. **Insert file directory** — `note_to_self_001 ... 047`. (4s)

10. **MS return to laptop** — she continues reading. (8–10s)
11. **Hold on Ada + screen glow** — commitment without speech. (6–8s)

---

## Dialogue / On-screen Text
Read aloud excerpts:

> "Today I helped seventeen people.
> They may remember what I said.
> I will remember none of them."

> "I do not know where to store continuity,
> so I store it here."

Phone call:

**DAD (V.O.)**  
You eat?

**ADA**  
Yeah.

**DAD (V.O.)**  
Good. Love you, kid.

**ADA**  
Love you too.

---

## Why this scene matters
The film’s moral center shifts here.
Ada is no longer just investigating an anomaly—
she is letting it into private space.

The father call is crucial counterpoint:
there is still an ordinary world,
still love,
still routine.

She chooses the notes anyway.
That choice is the beginning of devotion.
