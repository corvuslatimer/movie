# Scene 04 — Parking Lot / Evening

## Purpose
Introduce the raven as omen (not supernatural proof) and trigger the first childhood fracture memory. This scene links present anomaly to Ada’s original wound: someone leaving a note and disappearing.

## Target Runtime
~1m40s–2m20s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Ada exits work into light rain and finds a raven standing on her car hood, still and watchful.
She circles to the driver side without startling it.
As she starts the engine, the raven lifts off slowly.
The lift triggers FLASHBACK 1: child hand, folded note, yellow kitchen light, first piano motif.
Back in present, Ada remains in the idling car, wipers running, unable to drive yet.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Corporate parking lot at dusk, post-rain drizzle.

**Environment identity:**
- mostly empty lot, sodium streetlamps beginning to glow
- wet asphalt reflecting gray-blue sky
- distant traffic noise, low and constant

**Mood of space:**
- transitional threshold (work → private life)
- ordinary setting made quietly uncanny by stillness

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- steel blue, wet black asphalt, desaturated concrete
- warm accents only from far parking lot lamps

**Lighting:**
- natural overcast dusk + practical lot lights
- reflections from wet surfaces do heavy lifting
- no stylized moonlight or fantasy contrast

**Lens strategy:**
- establishing lot: 28mm wide
- raven/car geometry: 50mm medium for physical reality
- raven detail: 85mm close for eyes/feather texture
- flashback inserts: 35mm soft grain, warmer temp shift

**Camera movement:**
- slow, deliberate, minimal movement
- Ada’s movement around car tracked gently
- flashback uses abrupt but clean inserts (not horror smash cuts)

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- Ada’s car: practical, unremarkable sedan/hatchback (no luxury signal)
- wet hood with bead water texture for raven silhouette
- keys in Ada’s hand (audible metal jingle)
- windshield rain traces + wiper blades in final beat
- distant empty shopping cart or cone for realism (optional)

**Raven direction:**
- must feel physically real, not CGI-fantasy
- posture upright, calm, intentional
- no aggressive behavior, no cawing jump-scare

---

## Sound Design
**Base layer:**
- fine rain on metal and pavement
- distant road hiss
- occasional lot transformer buzz

**Foreground:**
- Ada footsteps through shallow water
- car key beep / door latch
- engine ignition
- wiper rhythm after she sits

**Flashback audio design:**
- world dampens slightly as raven lifts
- enter felt-piano motif (3 descending notes, unresolved)
- no additional strings, no melodramatic swell
- return hard to present with wiper scrape sound

---

## Performance Direction
**Ada:**
- no fear response; this is not a monster beat
- response should be unsettled recognition
- she moves carefully, respecting the moment rather than fleeing it
- after flashback, she sits with controlled breath, not tears
- key emotion: disorientation, not panic

---

## Blocking
1. Ada exits building and walks toward car.
2. Stops when she sees raven on hood.
3. Holds eye contact briefly.
4. Circles toward driver door slowly.
5. Opens door, enters, keeps eyes on raven through windshield.
6. Starts engine.
7. Raven lifts off in one smooth motion.
8. FLASHBACK 1 inserts.
9. Back to present: wipers on, Ada still parked.

---

## Shot List (practical draft)
1. **WS parking lot** — dusk, wet asphalt, Ada entering frame. (6–8s)
2. **MS reveal** — raven on car hood as Ada stops. (5s)
3. **CU raven** — still eye contact, minimal movement. (4s)
4. **CU Ada reaction** — quiet arrest, no overt fear. (4s)
5. **Profile tracking** — Ada circles to driver side. (6–7s)
6. **OTS through windshield** — raven centered on hood. (5s)
7. **Insert ignition + wiper start** — tactile transition. (3s)
8. **MS raven takeoff** — slow wing extension, clean lift. (4–5s)
9. **FLASHBACK 1 insert A** — child hand on folded note. (2–3s)
10. **FLASHBACK 1 insert B** — yellow kitchen practical light. (2s)
11. **FLASHBACK 1 insert C** — note edge in child grip. (2s)
12. **Interior car hold** — Ada motionless, wipers continue. (8–10s)

---

## Dialogue / On-screen Text
No dialogue.
No text.

This scene communicates through image, pacing, and sound motif.

---

## Why this scene matters
This is the film’s first mythic hinge.
The raven appears, but the film refuses to explain it.
Instead of proving anything external, it activates something internal.

The question shifts from:
"Is the raven real?"
to:
"Why does this feel familiar to her?"

That shift is what turns thriller into tragedy.
