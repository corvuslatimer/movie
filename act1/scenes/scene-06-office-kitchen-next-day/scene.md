# Scene 06 — Office Kitchen / Next Day

## Purpose
Introduce interpersonal pressure and social cost. Ada’s secrecy starts affecting real relationships. The line about a laptop “forgetting” lands like a knife because it mirrors the core tragedy.

## Target Runtime
~1m30s–2m10s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
In the office kitchen, Dara casually tells a story about her laptop crashing mid-work.
She says: “It just stopped. Like it forgot what it was doing.”
Ada freezes at the phrasing.
Dara notices and asks if she’s okay.
Ada gives a surface-level answer (“Just tired”).
Dara doesn’t buy it, but chooses care over confrontation and lets the moment pass.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Shared office kitchen, daytime.

**Space identity:**
- ordinary break-room functionality (microwave, sink, fridge, coffee station)
- minor clutter from real use (paper cups, dish rack, office snacks)

**Mood of space:**
- mundane and social
- emotionally low-stakes on the surface, high-stakes underneath
- this is where normal conversation accidentally collides with Ada’s private crisis

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- neutral off-whites, stainless steel, muted office tones
- less blue than server room, more human but still subdued

**Lighting:**
- overhead practicals + ambient daylight spill from hallway windows
- flat, realistic office lighting (no stylized contrast)

**Lens strategy:**
- two-shot conversation: 35mm/40mm
- emotional pinch (Ada freeze): 65mm/85mm close
- object inserts (mug, microwave timer, untouched food): 50mm

**Camera movement:**
- subtle handheld drift for conversational realism
- stabilize slightly at Ada freeze to emphasize internal lock

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- microwave with countdown timer visible
- kettle/coffee machine in background
- two mugs or disposable cups
- unopened snack or half-eaten lunch in front of Ada (she stops eating)
- fridge covered with generic office notices
- dish towel / sponge / normal kitchen residue (lived-in realism)

**Behavioral props:**
- Dara fiddles with coffee lid or spoon while speaking
- Ada keeps hands still when triggered

---

## Sound Design
**Base layer:**
- fridge compressor hum
- microwave beep in background (optional)
- distant office chatter through doorway

**Foreground:**
- cup set-down, spoon tap, chair shift
- Dara’s voice easy and conversational
- Ada’s silence should feel audible

**Music:**
- none

**Mix note:**
When Dara says “forgot what it was doing,” dip ambient just slightly for Ada’s subjective hit, then return to normal room tone when she answers.

---

## Performance Direction
**Dara:**
- warm, unforced, normal rhythm
- asks “you okay?” with real concern, not interrogation
- on Ada’s deflection, she reads the lie but chooses restraint

**Ada:**
- initially engaged enough to pass socially
- freeze is micro (half-second stillness, eye focus shift)
- reply should be automatic, practiced self-protection
- no dramatic breakdown; all pressure is internalized

---

## Blocking
1. Dara and Ada stand/sit in kitchen with coffee/lunch.
2. Dara tells laptop crash story while doing small kitchen motions.
3. Delivers line: “Like it forgot what it was doing.”
4. Ada freezes briefly.
5. Dara clocks it and asks: “You okay?”
6. Ada deflects: “Yeah. Just tired.”
7. Dara holds eye contact one beat, then nods and lets it go.
8. Conversation shifts or scene exits without resolution.

---

## Shot List (practical draft)
1. **WS kitchen establish** — office normality, people passing outside. (5–6s)
2. **MS two-shot** — Dara mid-story, light conversational energy. (8s)
3. **Insert Ada’s untouched food/mug** — subtle stress signal. (3s)
4. **CU Dara line** — “Like it forgot what it was doing.” (3–4s)
5. **CU Ada freeze** — tiny stillness + blink delay. (3–4s)
6. **MS Dara concern beat** — “You okay?” (3s)
7. **CU Ada deflect** — “Yeah. Just tired.” (3s)
8. **Two-shot hold** — Dara doesn’t believe it, lets it pass. (5–6s)
9. **Exit/linger** — mundane room tone resumes dominance. (4–5s)

---

## Why this scene matters
This is the first social consequence scene.
Ada’s secret is no longer contained to terminals and private files—it’s now shaping how she relates to living people.

Dara’s restraint is key.
She sees the fracture and does not force it open.
That makes her care feel real, and raises the cost of Ada’s silence.
