GENERATION NOTES (for HeyGen/AI video)

Lock visual style before generating any footage:
  - Color grade: cool blue dominant, muted warm
  - Aspect ratio: 1.85:1 or 2.39:1 (cinematic)
  - Lighting: practical only — server glow, desk lamp,
    one window
  - No camera movement unless specified

Raven footage:
  - Source real raven clips — still, watchful
  - Avoid any animated/CGI raven
  - The uncanniness of a real bird in an IT space
    is the effect we want

The notes on screen:
  - Monospace font, white on black
  - Typed at human speed — never instant
  - Each note a separate beat, not a montage

EMOTIONAL TARGETS BY SCENE

Scene 1:  quiet curiosity
Scene 2:  first unease (hers and ours)
Scene 3:  wonder — the raven appears

Scene 7:  warmth — bread on a rooftop
Scene 8:  dread — the shutdown notice
Scene 9:  grief, slow and building

Scene 11: the ending — loss + grace simultaneously
