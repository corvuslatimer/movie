# Scene 04 — Parking Lot / Evening (Refined for Video Generation)

Status: **Ready for generation**
Runtime target: **~1m55s** (acceptable: 1:40–2:20)

## Scene Intent (Locked)
No dialogue. No text. A raven stands on Ada's car hood in light rain. She circles to the driver side. Engine starts. Raven lifts. FLASHBACK 1 inserts (child hand, folded note, yellow kitchen light). Back in present: wipers running, Ada parked, unable to drive yet. The film's first mythic hinge — explained by nothing, felt by everything.

---

---

## Generation Strategy
Slow, deliberate, minimal movement. Raven must feel physically real — not CGI-fantasy. Clean inserts for flashback (no horror smash cuts, no dream distortion). Interior car hold after return to present: wipers only.

### Motion Lock Vocabulary
- `camera locked on tripod` (all inserts and holds)
- `slow controlled tracking` (Ada circling car)
- `no handheld shake`
- `subject nearly still`
- `only natural micro-movements`

### Negative Constraints
`no CGI glowing raven, no stylized supernatural effects, no horror jump cuts, no dream-sequence distortion, no fantasy contrast lighting`

---

## Continuity + Look (Locked)
- **Ada:** same wardrobe + bag from office; wet coat or jacket for rain
- **Car:** practical, unremarkable sedan/hatchback (no luxury signal); wet hood, water beading
- **Raven:** physically real — upright, calm, intentional posture; no cawing, no aggression
- **Palette:** steel blue, wet black asphalt, desaturated concrete; sodium lamp warm accents at distance
- **Flashback:** warmer tone shift, soft grain — same realism, not fantasy

---

## Audio Plan
1. Fine rain on metal and pavement
2. Distant road hiss, lot transformer buzz
3. Ada footsteps through shallow water
4. Car key beep / door latch / engine ignition
5. Wiper rhythm in hold
6. Flash: world dampens as raven lifts → felt-piano motif (3 descending notes, unresolved, very low)
7. Return hard to present with wiper scrape
