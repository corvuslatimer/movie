# Scene 02 — Office Floor / Noon (Refined for Video Generation)

Status: **Ready for generation**
Runtime target: **~2m00s** (acceptable: 1:45–2:20)

---

## Scene Intent (Locked)
Fate is announced in office language. Dara drops coffee, exchanges pleasantries. Marcus posts routine refresh in team chat. Ada opens the ticket, reads "Rebuild: Yes / Migration: No," reads the policy banner underneath, holds one beat too long, and minimizes fast. The violence is bureaucratic. No one notices.

## Generation Strategy
Gentle handheld or slider drift for human warmth. Stabilize on ticket reveal beat. Monitor inserts must be legible on phone in one pass.

### Motion Lock Vocabulary
- `camera locked on tripod` (inserts)
- `gentle handheld drift` (conversation — very subtle, not shaky)
- `no handheld shake`
- `subject nearly still`
- `text remains sharp and readable throughout`

### Negative Constraints
`no glitch effects, no stylized sci-fi neon, no dramatic push-ins, no chromatic aberration, no sci-fi UI`

---

## Continuity + Look (Locked)
- **Ada:** 32, dark brown tied hair, light olive skin, navy/gray wardrobe, two monitors, badge visible
- **Dara:** warm presence, two takeaway coffees with sleeves, messenger bag
- **Environment:** open-plan corporate tech floor, midday daylight + soft overhead, multiple monitors, lived-in desk clutter, slightly dying office plant in background
- **Marcus:** appears as chat notification only — no on-screen appearance

---

## Audio Plan
1. Soft office murmur, distant keyboard chatter, HVAC
2. Coffee cup placed on desk (small thud)
3. Notification ping — ordinary, not ominous
4. Mouse click as Ada opens ticket
5. When Ada reads "Migration: No": ambient dips briefly (1 second), then restores

## Assembly Notes
- Hard cuts only
- Clip 06 (ticket fields) — hold each field long enough to read on phone
- Clip 07 (reaction beat) is the scene's pivot — do not cut short
- Office energy should feel warmly familiar; the danger is in the ticket, not the room

## Acceptance Test
Pass if viewer understands:
1. The normal social world (Dara, office warmth)
2. The fate was just announced in mundane language
3. Ada saw the danger in the ticket and looked away fast
