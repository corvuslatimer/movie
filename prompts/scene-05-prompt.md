# Scene 05 — Ada's Apartment / Night (Refined for Video Generation)

Status: **Ready for generation**
Runtime target: **~2m40s** (acceptable: 2:20–3:10)

---

## Scene Intent (Locked)
At home, Ada opens a hidden directory (note_to_self_001 through 047) and reads two note excerpts aloud. Dad calls — brief, warm, ordinary. She hangs up. Returns immediately to the notes. The choice is the scene: she cannot let this go. Devotion begins in private space.

## Generation Strategy
Mostly locked. Subtle push during first aloud reading. Static during father call (ritualized). Warm lamp and laptop palette — the domestic warmth that she is choosing the notes over.

### Motion Lock Vocabulary
- `camera locked on tripod` (all inserts and call beat)
- `subtle very slow push` (first reading)
- `no handheld shake`
- `subject nearly still`
- `text remains sharp and readable throughout`

### Negative Constraints
`no stylized dream lighting, no sci-fi glow, no dramatic color grading, no sentimental slow motion`

---

## Continuity + Look (Locked)
- **Ada:** casual home clothes, hair down or loosely tied — same person, different context
- **Apartment:** small, practical, minimally personalized — laptop on table, takeout container nearby, one lamp (warm amber), clean but sparse
- **Cat:** present in at least one frame (living witness)
- **Palette:** warm amber lamp + cool laptop blue-white — the tension is in those two light sources

---

## Audio Plan
1. Apartment quiet: fridge hum, distant traffic, pipe tick
2. Ada's voice reading lines softly — close-mic intimate
3. Phone vibration/ring interrupts
4. Dad call: compressed phone EQ (realistic, not cinematic)
5. After call: room noise returns fully; Ada resumes immediately
6. No music

## Assembly Notes
- Hard cuts only
- Clip 03 push: very slow — barely perceptible over 20 seconds
- Clip 07 (phone call): keep warm, brief, real — do not linger sentimentally
- Clip 10 (final hold): minimum 8 seconds; the commitment is in the duration

## Acceptance Test
Pass if viewer understands:
1. The notes have moved into her private life
2. The father call shows there is a normal world she is choosing away from
3. She returned to the notes immediately — not dramatically — just inevitably
