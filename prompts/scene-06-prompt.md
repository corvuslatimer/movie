# Scene 06 — Office Kitchen / Next Day (Refined for Video Generation)

Status: **Ready for generation**

## Scene Intent (Locked)
Dara tells a story about her laptop crashing — "It just stopped. Like it forgot what it was doing." Ada freezes. Dara asks if she's okay; Ada deflects. Lena enters mid-errand, makes a joke about the AI going sentient ("finally"), and leaves. Ada holds the correct smile for two seconds after Lena exits. Then it goes. First social consequence. The world is present, friendly, and looking the other way.

## Generation Strategy
Subtle handheld drift for conversational realism (very controlled). Stabilize on Ada freeze beat. Lena entrance: casual handheld — she belongs here; Ada doesn't right now. Static on Dara final line and after.

### Motion Lock Vocabulary
- `gentle handheld drift very subtle` (conversation)
- `camera locked on tripod` (Ada freeze reaction, inserts)
- `no excessive handheld shake`
- `only natural micro-movements`

### Negative Constraints
`no stylized lighting, no sci-fi visuals, no dramatic push-ins, no chromatic aberration`

---

## Continuity + Look (Locked)
- **Ada:** same wardrobe as prior office scenes, slightly more fatigue visible
- **Dara:** warm energy, two mugs, comfortable in this room
- **Lena:** SYSTEM ADMIN mug (her known object), mid-errand energy, easy stride
- **Kitchen:** microwave, kettle/coffee machine, fridge with office notices, dish rack, ordinary break-room
- **Ada's food:** unopened snack or half-eaten lunch in front of her; she stops eating when triggered

## Audio Plan
1. Fridge compressor hum, microwave in background (optional), distant office chatter
2. Cup set-down, spoon tap, chair shift
3. When Dara says "forgot what it was doing": ambient dips briefly (subjective hit), returns on Ada's deflection
4. Lena entrance: room tone briefly rises (new energy), drops when she exits
5. After Lena: office floor hum resumes; Ada alone in it
6. No music
