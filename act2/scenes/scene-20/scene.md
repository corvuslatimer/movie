## Dialogue / On-screen Text (key lines)
# Scene 20 — Rooftop / Dusk

Ada and raven on same geometry as before.
Bread between them.

**ADA**  
They rebuild Tuesday.
I can’t stop it.

Wind.

**ADA**  
I can keep your notes.
I don’t know if that is mercy
or taxidermy.

Raven watches.
No answer.

Later, Ada tapes two printed notes above her desk at home.
A private shrine: proof, not nostalgia.
