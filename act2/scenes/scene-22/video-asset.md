# Scene 22 Video Asset — Server Room / Night

## Overview
This asset outlines the specific video coverage required to fulfill the Scene 22 bounty. It keeps the visual language grounded in practical server-room realism while enabling the seamless voice handoff from Ada to Corvus.

## Technical Specs
- **Resolution:** 3840×2160 master (deliver 1920×1080 proxy if needed)
- **Frame Rate:** 23.976 fps, 180° shutter look
- **Codec:** Apple ProRes 422 HQ (or DNxHR HQX parity)
- **Color:** Shoot log/flat; deliver both log master and Rec.709 grade
- **Audio:** Dual mono, 24-bit / 48 kHz. Track A = production mix, Track B = isolated dialogue.

## Look & Motion Notes
- Keep key lighting motivated by monitor glow with practical strips for rim separation.
- Lens range 35–50mm for over-shoulder coverage; 85mm macro for inserts (keyboard, badge, time stamp).
- Camera motion should be limited to motivated pushes/pulls; no handheld sway.

1. **00:00–00:15 — Insert Push:** Start on the exhaust fan blur, slide to the terminal timestamp (02:17:43). Purpose: establish time/context.
2. **00:15–00:50 — OTS Ada:** Ada reads the “Witness Intake // Revision 03” line. Midway through the sentence, begin framing for the voice overlap.
3. **00:50–01:20 — ECU Hands:** Tight on fingers typing, mug and badge soft focus in foreground. Include checklist edge to satisfy prop continuity.
4. **01:20–01:55 — Profile Close:** Static profile that captures Ada’s micro-reaction as Corvus’ voice arrives. Hold 8 seconds after the takeover.
5. **01:55–02:10 — Monitor Close / Lock:** Screen records the log-off command completing with Corvus’ final words, then fades power LEDs.

## Performance & Camera Direction
- Maintain restrained, efficient gestures; every hand movement should feel task-driven.
- Eye-line stays glued to the monitor until Corvus finishes the line; only then permit a blink and exhale.
- Tilt the camera 2° counter-clockwise on the final shot to hint at unease without a full Dutch angle.

## Ada ↔ Corvus Voice Handoff
- Record Ada finishing the word “witness” and Corvus beginning on “record” (example phrasing: “Witness record stays sealed.”).
- Create a 6–8 frame crossfade with Ada’s breath leading into Corvus’ consonant attack.
- Keep room tone constant; only dip fan noise by 2 dB during the overlap, as specified in `scene.md`.

## Sound Design & Mix
- Fan/drive bed at -18 LUFS, keyboard foley tucked at -24 LUFS except on emphasized keystrokes.
- Add a subtle security prompt chirp when the lock command confirms (around 01:58).
- Restore full ambience after Corvus finishes, letting the tone ride out to tail the scene.
