# note_to_self - Visual Style Guide (Dreamcore Update)

## Overall Aesthetic
**Dreamcore liminal dread:** Grain-heavy VHS/35mm emulation (Kodak 5219 + NTSC warp). Desaturated cool blues/greys (#1E3A5F to #94A3B8)—expansive empty server aisles/halls evoke endless isolation. Warm yellow intrusions (#F1C40F to #D97706) fracture reality in flashbacks/apt (dream haze bleed). Subtle uncanny: Distant figures vanish on cut, reflections lag 1 frame, audio-visual desync hints.

**Lighting:** Harsh fluorescents flicker (server: infinite loop hum), sodium vapor rooftops (endless dusk), apt pools of sodium lamp glow. Expansive shadows stretch unnaturally.

**Camera:** 2.39:1 ultra-wide. Locked-off vast emptiness (dreamcore scale), creeping handheld (personal fracture). Slow dollies into voids. Lens flares ethereal (no Hollywood polish).

**Texture:** Heavy grain/distortion (VHS tracking lines, film burns). Shallow DoF isolates faces/notes in infinite space. Rack focus: Terminal → vanishing horizon.
