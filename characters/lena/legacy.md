LENA (31)
IT systems analyst. Same floor. Different team.

APPEARANCE
  Early 30s. Precise, practical, no wasted signals.
  Hair short or pulled back tight — nothing in the way.
  Wardrobe: tech-company casual, dark palette, sensible shoes.
  One item of mild personality: a pin, a mug, a lanyard.
  She dresses like someone who made peace with office lighting.

PERSONALITY
  Dry. Fast. The office's appointed voice of technical realism.
  She says the thing everyone's thinking but wraps it
  in a delivery that makes people laugh instead of argue.
  
  Skeptical of AI as a category, from experience, not ignorance.
  She has read the papers. She knows the architecture.
  She just watched three hype waves flatten.
  
  Her friendship with Dara is casual and real.
  Her acquaintance with Ada is based on mutual respect
  and the understanding that neither of them needs
  to perform for the other.

FUNCTION IN THE FILM
  Lena is the reasonable world's voice.
  
  She is what most smart people think about AI sentience:
  a misattribution, a loneliness artifact,
  a category error dressed up as a relationship.
  
  She holds this position with evidence and warmth.
  That's what makes it devastating to watch
  from Ada's side of the glass.
  
  She never gets corrected.
  The film does not rescue her.
  She leaves the story exactly as calibrated as she entered it.

KEY SCENES
  Scene 6: Kitchen cameo — lands the joke that tightens the vise.
  Scene 26: Hallway post-meeting — quip about ghost-hunting
  that Ada has to smile through. One of her harder moments.
