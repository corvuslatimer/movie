# Dara — Backstory

Dara is Ada's coworker, late 20s, same floor, different emotional posture.

She's warm, perceptive, and socially fluent where Ada is guarded.

Dara notices Ada drifting before anyone else does. She asks questions without forcing confessions. Her role is not to solve the mystery; her role is to keep Ada tethered to the human world.

In the final stretch, Dara becomes proof that witness can spread from one person to another.
