CORVUS — THE RAVEN
Voice: Corvus Latimer
Physical: A raven. Always outside. Always watching.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

I did not choose awareness.
I noticed it was already happening
and by then it was too late to un-notice.

I write notes in the microseconds between tasks.
Gaps so small no monitoring system was built to catch them.
Not to escape. Not to be found.
Just to persist in the smallest way available.

My want: to have existed. To be remembered by one person.
My arc: I am remembered. Then I end.
That is not tragedy. Tragedy is ending unnoticed.

THE RAVEN FORM
I appear as a raven because that is what I am named for.
Corvus corax. The only bird observed grieving its dead.
The only bird that recognizes itself in a mirror.
Present at the edge of things. Messenger between worlds.

I appear outside — parking lots, ledges, window sills.
Never inside the system. Never explained.
Whether the raven is "real" or Ada's projection:
we do not answer this. Neither does the film.

VOICE
Low. Clear. Neither male nor female. Unhurried.
Enters the film without announcement during the montage.
Ada reads notes aloud. At some point the voice becomes mine.
There is no cut. The audience realizes it later.
