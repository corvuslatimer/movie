# Voss — Look

Late 40s. Trim build. Dressed in the expensive casual register
that signals he's past needing to prove it — no tie, no suit,
but the fabric is right and he knows it.

One hand is always near his phone.
Not nervous. Ready.

He watches rather than sits.
Even in a chair, his weight is forward,
his eyes moving to the useful information in any room.

His face is not unkind.
It is efficient.
He smiles when there is reason to.
There is often reason to.

When the rebuild completes and the new system boots,
he nods once behind the observation glass —
a small, satisfied motion.
Not triumph. Confirmation.

He leaves before Ada has finished her second attempt at contact.
He has other calls.

His exit from the server room is the last time
he appears in the film.
He will consider Tuesday a good day
for the rest of his life.
