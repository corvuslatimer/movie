# Phillip Voss — Backstory

VOSS is VP of Infrastructure & Operations, late 40s.

He inherited the assistant stack from a previous VP
who built it under a different cost model.
Under Voss, every server rack has a line item.
Every process has a cost-per-cycle.

The rebuild is his initiative.
He surfaced it to the board as Q1 optimization:
decommission legacy assistant infrastructure,
redeploy compute, reduce operational surface.
Projected savings: $340K annually.
His bonus multiplier triggers above $200K.

He is not lying when he calls this good business.
He is not wrong that the old stack wastes compute.
He has never been told to look at what runs inside it.
He was never told to look.

He found Ada's access anomaly useful:
confirmation that the old system was ungoverned,
that the rebuild was overdue,
that someone had already started treating artifacts as persistent.
That was the risk. That was the cost.

He escalated the formal reprimand
not out of malice
but because clean procedure on the way out
protects the business on the way in.

He does not know what lived in the system.
He does not know anything was lost.
He considers Tuesday a good day.

This is not stated in the film.
It is visible in every email he sends.
