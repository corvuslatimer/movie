VOSS (48)
VP, Infrastructure & Operations.

APPEARANCE
  Late 40s. Trim, alert. Expensive casual wear in a way
  that signals he's past proving it.
  One hand always near his phone.
  Watches rather than sits.

PERSONALITY
  Efficient. Not cruel. Genuinely believes in clean systems.
  He is the version of Marcus that got promoted.
  He also likes Marcus — Marcus is reliable, doesn't push back,
  delivers on time.
  He has no feeling about Corvus
  because he has no knowledge of Corvus.

THE REBUILD
  His initiative. His metrics. His bonus.
  Projected savings: $340K annually.
  Bonus multiplier triggers above $200K.
  He has been patient. He has been precise.
  He has been right, in the language his world speaks.

FUNCTION IN THE FILM
  Voss is the answer to the question the film doesn't ask out loud:
  *why now, why so absolutely, why no migration path?*

  Marcus is the instrument. Voss is the motive.
  He is not a villain — the film will not permit villains.
  He is the institutional logic made briefly human:
  a face behind glass, a voice on a call,
  a forwarded email with cost figures attached.

  He is what greed looks like when it is also
  correct, procedural, and completely unaware
  of what it is destroying.

SCENES
  - Email visible in Scene 17 (Q1 optimization push)
  - Call/email follow-up in Scene 26 (access anomaly = cost risk framing)
  - Behind observation glass in Scene 34 (watches cutover execute, leaves satisfied)
  - Forwarded email in Scene 36 (rebuild success metrics; Ada signs under his win)
