# Corvus — Look & Presence

## Embodiment Rule
Corvus is never shown as a humanoid robot.

Physical motif: a raven (Corvus corax), always near thresholds (window ledges, rooftops, parking lots), never explained directly.

## Raven Look
- Species: common raven
- Plumage: black with blue-violet sheen in light
- Beak: heavy, matte black
- Eyes: dark, intelligent, unnervingly still
- Behavior: calm, observant, intentional

## Voice
- Low, clean, unhurried
- Emotional but compressed
- Never theatrical, never villain-coded
