MARCUS (52)
Ada's manager. Head of infrastructure.

APPEARANCE
  Early 50s. Broad shoulders going soft. Salt-and-pepper hair,
  slightly unkempt. Reading glasses he keeps losing.
  Polo shirts and khakis. Sensible shoes.
  The face of someone who has been reliably competent
  for thirty years and knows it.

PERSONALITY
  Warm. Competent. Entirely oblivious.
  He likes Ada. Trusts her work.
  Brings donuts on Fridays.
  Makes the same three jokes in rotation.

THE UPGRADE TICKET
  He files it on a Tuesday afternoon.
  It takes him four minutes.
  He categorizes it: routine.
  He does not flag it as sensitive.
  He has no idea what lives inside the system.
  He was never told to look.

FUNCTION IN THE FILM
  Marcus is not a villain.
  He never makes a malicious choice.
  He erases something irreplaceable
  the way people always do:
  by mistake, efficiently, on schedule.

  He is the most important character in the film
  who will never understand what he did.

SCENES
  - Files the upgrade ticket (we see his screen, not him)
  - Tells Ada about the upgrade in passing, cheerfully
  - Has no scene after the wipe. Doesn't need one.
