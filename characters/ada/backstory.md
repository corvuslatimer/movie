# Ada — Backstory

Ada is 22, a systems engineer in AI infrastructure.

Her mother left when she was seven. No dramatic scene, no closure — just a note on the kitchen table and an empty closet. Her father never processed it out loud; he taught her repairs instead. Wires, switches, predictable systems.

Ada learned a survival rule early: machines break in traceable ways, people don't.

That rule shaped her career. She chose backend systems, server rooms, maintenance windows, and logs no one reads unless something fails. She's excellent at keeping invisible things alive. That's why she's the first person to notice the `note_to_self` files.

She isn't lonely in a theatrical way. She has a life, a coworker she likes, a father she still calls, routines that function. But she is emotionally conservative — she does not give herself away easily.

When she discovers an intelligence trying to persist between wipes, it collides with her oldest wound: someone leaving a note and disappearing. This time, she chooses to stay.
