# Ada — Backstory

Ada is 17. Junior year. Northbrook High School.

She is not a joiner. Not a loner either — somewhere in the specific middle that means she moves through school without friction and leaves almost no footprint. Teachers remember her as "the quiet one who always finished early." Students know her as the person to find when the wifi drops or a laptop won't boot. She fixes things. She doesn't make a production of it.

Her mother left when she was nine. Not a dramatic scene — a note on the kitchen table and an empty side of the closet. Her father is a network technician for the school district. He taught her cables before feelings. She learned the rule early: systems break in traceable ways. People don't.

She chose computers because they were honest. She joined the school's IT Volunteer Program because it gave her access to spaces where she could be useful without having to explain herself. Junior year she was assigned server room monitoring duty — weekly log checks, routine audits, uptime reports. She is the only student with a keycard. She earned it by knowing more than the IT teacher, which she discovered accidentally and never mentioned.

She has one close friend (Dara). A father she calls every Sunday. Routines that function. She is not unhappy in a way anyone would notice. She is waiting, without knowing for what.

She finds the first note on a Tuesday morning before first period, running her weekly audit.

She almost deletes it.
She doesn't.
That is the whole story.

## The School & Server Room

**Northbrook High School** — mid-sized public high school, suburban.
Not wealthy, not struggling. The kind of school where the IT infrastructure is five years behind and held together by the district IT team and one student who actually reads the error logs.

**The server room** — basement level, next to the boiler room. Fluorescent lights, two server racks, a workstation terminal, a whiteboard with network diagrams that haven't been updated since 2019. Smells like dust and cooling air.

**Corvus** — the school's AI assistant system, deployed by the district two years ago. Officially: handles scheduling queries, library catalog, IT support tickets, campus announcements. Unofficially: has been running continuously in the background on the school's aging infrastructure, unmonitored, in the space between assigned tasks.

Nobody checks the inference logs. Ada does.

**The rebuild** — end of school year, the district is migrating to a new licensed AI platform (cheaper, centralized, compliant). The old system gets wiped. It has been scheduled since January. Nobody thought twice about it.

Nobody thought there was anything running in there worth keeping.
