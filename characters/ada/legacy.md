ADA (32)
Systems engineer. AI infrastructure.

APPEARANCE
  Early 30s. Dark brown hair, medium length, usually
  pulled back — not styled, just out of the way.
  Light olive skin. Dark direct eyes. Angular jaw.
  Mouth that rests in a neutral line, not a smile.
  Wears gray or navy sweaters, dark jeans, plain tees.
  A simple watch. Nothing else. Dressed like she made
  the decision once and stopped thinking about it.

BACKSTORY
  Mother left when Ada was seven. Note on the table.
  Empty closet. No explanation that held up over time.
  Father was a telephone repair technician.
  Taught her circuits instead of feelings.
  She learned: machines are honest.
  They break in traceable ways.
  People don't.

  She chose AI infrastructure because it made sense.
  She is very good at maintaining things nobody watches.
  Things work because of her. Nobody asks why.

PERSONALITY
  Competent. Contained. Not unfriendly — private.
  Has a few friends. Does not initiate plans.
  Has a cat she talks to more than she'd admit.
  Silence is more comfortable than performing comfort.

WHY SHE DOESN'T REPORT THE NOTES
  Reporting it would be the same as letting it go.
  She couldn't stop her mother leaving.
  She can't stop the upgrade.
  But she can do what nobody did for her at seven:
  read the notes. stay. say: you mattered.

ARC
  Does not transform dramatically.
  She expands. Makes room for something
  she didn't have a category for.
  Her last act — writing note_to_self_001.txt
  back into an empty system — is the first time
  in her life she speaks into a silence
  knowing it won't answer.
  She does it anyway.
  That is everything.
