# Ada — Look

ADA (17)
High school junior. IT Volunteer Program. Northbrook High.

APPEARANCE
  17. Dark brown hair, medium length, usually in a loose tie
  or pulled through a hoodie collar — not styled, just managed.
  Light olive skin. Dark direct eyes. The kind of face
  that looks older when she's concentrating, younger when she laughs.
  Wears hoodies, dark jeans, worn sneakers.
  One silver stud in each ear. Nothing else.
  Dressed like comfort is the only decision she makes.

  She carries a backpack that's always slightly too full.
  Always has a cable or a small screwdriver somewhere in it.

MOVEMENT
  Quiet. Efficient. She doesn't fill space she doesn't need.
  In the server room she moves differently — slower, more careful,
  like she knows the room and the room knows her back.

PRESENCE
  Not invisible — precise. She is exactly as present as she chooses to be.
  People trust her with things they can't explain. She doesn't ask why.
