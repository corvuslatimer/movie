## Recording Notes

### Corvus V.O.
- Recorded clean, no processing at source
- Post: subtle warmth saturation only — not robotic, not synthetic
- Delivery: precise sentence rhythm, slight pause before final clause
- Emotional register: curiosity > observation > care > fear (in that order through the film)
- Never performs sadness; only states it

### Ada readings (ADA-READ)
- These are Ada voicing Corvus's notes aloud — testing what they mean by saying them
- Should feel tentative at first (Sc 5), then intimate (Sc 16), then fluent (Sc 22)
- The transition from ADA-READ to CORVUS in Scene 22 is the film's formal voice handoff

### Dad (V.O.)
- Compressed phone EQ — realistic, not cinematic
- Warmth without sentiment; years of this exact cadence
- He loves her and has no idea how to reach her
