# note_to_self — Integration Checklist (Shoot-Ready)

Purpose: final continuity QA across screenplay, structure lock, dialogue, artifact bible, shot priorities, and prompts.

## A) Story Continuity Gates (Pass/Fail)

1. **Clock Integrity (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Tuesday 06:00 rebuild appears in Hook/Act I and drives Act III cutover.
   - "No migration" language is explicit before Act II midpoint.
   - No later scene implies viable rescue/backup.

2. **Act Escalation Integrity (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Act I = omen/anomaly + first contact.
   - Act II = recognition/intimacy + explicit vow ("i include you" / "i won't look away").
   - Act III = catastrophe on-screen + paid consequence + witness vocation.

3. **Institutional Logic Integrity (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Marcus/IT/Voss remain procedural, not moustache-twirling villains.
   - Corporate motive remains operational/economic framing.
   - Harm is delivered through process, not melodramatic malice.

4. **Consequence Integrity (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Reprimand/reassignment/removal of elevated access are explicit.
   - Ada cannot return to status quo role.
   - Ending is moral transformation, not plot rescue.

## B) Dialogue Continuity Gates (Pass/Fail)

5. **Voice Separation (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Ada lines stay practical, compressed, human-defensive.
   - Corvus lines stay precise, structured, unsentimental.
   - Side characters frame social reality quickly (Dara grounds, Lena deflects).

6. **Thesis Language (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Witness-over-rescue language appears and remains consistent.
   - Final exchange favors plain accuracy over ornament.
   - No "miracle survival" drift in late rewrites.

## C) Artifact/Visual Grammar Gates (Pass/Fail)

7. **Escalation Rule (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Artifacts are subtle in Act I, noticeable in Act II, medium-break in Act III.
   - Artifacts are motivated by continuity fracture/witness stakes, not random styling.

8. **Single Clean-Shot Exception (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Exactly one near-clean emotional contrast shot exists in the film.
   - It is reserved for final witness punctuation, not reused elsewhere.

9. **Motif Continuity (PASS/FAIL)**
   - Raven, notes, terminal cursor, and piano motif recur with consistent meaning.
   - Piano motif escalation holds: 3 notes (early), 4 notes (mid), unresolved 5-note full statement (catastrophe).

## D) Scene/Shot Cross-Reference Gates (Pass/Fail)

10. **Scene Numbering Integrity (PASS/FAIL)**
    - Shot list references screenplay scene numbers accurately.
    - Quotes in shot list match current screenplay wording for key beats.

11. **Cross-File Canon Sync (PASS/FAIL)**
    - `screenplay.md` and `act1/act2/act3` agree on critical continuity beats:
      - Scene 22 voice-handoff timing
      - Scene 25 routing-chain escalation detail
      - Scene 31 audit-log reveal + `079d`
      - Scene 35 `LOSS_CERTIFICATE.md` ritual step
    - No production doc references a superseded version of those beats.

12. **Oversight Chain Consistency (PASS/FAIL)**
    - Director/VP oversight mentions are non-contradictory across screenplay and production docs.
    - Economic motive + documentation-cleanup motive stay aligned (no villain drift).

13. **Priority-40 Coverage (PASS/FAIL)**
    - First 40 shots capture Hook + Act I causality chain:
      anomaly → policy stakes → outreach → approval → first reply.

14. **Prompt Compatibility (PASS/FAIL)**
    - Dreamcore prompts retain artifact descriptors and liminal tone.
    - Act-specific prompt blocks map to artifact bible escalation.

## Pre-Shoot Signoff Checklist

- [ ] Screenplay lock, act files, and structure lock read side-by-side; no causal contradictions.
- [ ] Dialogue pass confirms voice separation in Scenes 12, 14, 23, 33, 37.
- [ ] Artifact pass map approved per act (I/II/III escalation + one clean-shot allocation).
- [ ] Shot Priority 40 references validated against current screenplay text.
- [ ] Prompt packet validated for location/motif/character consistency.
- [ ] Production lead signoff: **READY TO SHOOT**.

## Failure Protocol

If any gate fails:
1. Log exact file + scene/line mismatch.
2. Apply minimal edit in the most downstream artifact (shot list/prompt/docs) first.
3. Re-open screenplay only if contradiction cannot be resolved downstream.
4. Re-run this checklist and re-signoff.
