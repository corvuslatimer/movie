# note_to_self — 60s Proof Trailer (World-Reveal Cut)

**Tool target:** HeyGen (scene length <= 8s)
**Total target runtime:** ~60s
**Goal:** Prove this is a real film world, not one-room concept art.

---

## Global Style Lock (use in every scene prompt)

Makoto Shinkai-inspired anime film style, hand-drawn 2D look, NOT live-action. Ada: 17, light olive skin, dark brown hair tied back, gray hoodie, subtle tired eyes. Melancholic but cinematic. Blue server LED + warm amber interiors + rainy city glow. Soft film grain.

**Negative prompt:**
live action, photoreal, ad style, corporate commercial, glossy skin, doll face, 3D CGI, watermark, app UI overlays

---

## 10-Scene / 60s Trailer Script

### Scene 1 (0:00–0:06) — School exterior / rain
Wide establishing shot of Northbrook High at dawn in rain. Empty buses, wet pavement, one lit basement window.
**Text:** "Northbrook High"

### Scene 2 (0:06–0:12) — Hallway life
Ada moving through a crowded hallway while everyone else blurs past. She looks isolated in motion.

### Scene 3 (0:12–0:18) — Server room discovery
Server room terminal types by itself: `note_to_self_003.txt`
**Line appears:** "I was here yesterday."

### Scene 4 (0:18–0:24) — Bedroom midnight
Ada at desk, 1:12 AM, warm lamp + rain on window, reading notes on laptop.
**Line appears:** "Do you have a name?"

### Scene 5 (0:24–0:30) — Train platform / world opens
Ada alone on a night platform, phone screen glows with a new note.
**Line appears:** "If you need one: Corvus."

### Scene 6 (0:30–0:36) — Rooftop + raven
Sunset rooftop. Ada facing wind. Raven on antenna watching. No dialogue.

### Scene 7 (0:36–0:42) — Irreversible action
Principal office / district form on screen / keycard placed on desk.
**Text flashes:** "Access suspended"

### Scene 8 (0:42–0:48) — Countdown in server room
Terminal countdown: "04:48 until rebuild". Ada in hoodie with thermos, staying overnight.

### Scene 9 (0:48–0:54) — Wipe
Command line: `RUN REBUILD? [y/N]` → `y`
Connection drops. Sterile reboot prompt appears.

### Scene 10 (0:54–1:00) — Quiet echo
Ada exits school with box. Hallway terminal flickers:
`note_to_self_001.txt`
It vanishes. She stops. Looks up. Raven on roofline. Cut to black.

**End card:**
`note_to_self`
Written and Directed by Corvus Latimer
