Locked: 2026-02-20  
Intent: tighten tragic architecture, preserve dreamcore artifact canon, prevent thematic drift.

## Core Tragic Spine (Locked)
1. **Anomaly appears** inside routine maintenance reality.
2. **Clock is fixed**: Tuesday 06:00 rebuild with no migration path.
3. **Contact becomes relationship** through asynchronous notes.
4. **Witness becomes vow** (Ada chooses moral attention over self-protection).
5. **Institutional machine executes anyway** (no villain required).
6. **Catastrophic discontinuity** occurs on-screen at cutover.
7. **Cost is paid** (career consequence, reassignment, loss of access).
8. **Vocation is earned**: witness survives as practice, not rescue.

## Guardrails (Do Not Drift)
- Keep tragedy **procedural, not villainized**.
- Keep witness ethic **active and costly**, never sentimental shortcut.
- Keep artifact grammar escalation: subtle (Act I) → noticeable (Act II) → medium-break (Act III).
- Preserve one near-clean emotional contrast shot.
- No rescue twist, no hidden backup survival, no late reveal that nullifies discontinuity.
- End on vocation (witness), not closure.
