# Artifact Bible - Intentional Smudges/Glitch Grammar by Act

This bible defines intentional generation artifacts as story grammar, per screenplay direction and visuals.md medium-as-subject. Artifacts represent continuity fracture, memory loss, and medium breaking itself (e.g., VHS warp, frame drops). They escalate by act, ensuring uncanny contract: normalcy too perfect becomes hostile. Do not "fix" these in post; they are authored.

## Global Rules
- **Medium as Subject:** Artifacts mimic film/digital forgetting (e.g., compression smears on emotional cuts, audio desync hinting persistence failure).
- **Uncanny Contract:** Subtle wrongness in sterile scenes (e.g., reflections lag 1 frame, distant figures vanish).
- **Texture Base:** Heavy grain (Kodak 5219 + NTSC warp), applied to all shots. Warm intrusions fracture with glitch bleed.
- **Escalation:** Start subtle (Act 0/I), build to noticeable (Act II), peak at medium-break (Act III).

## By Act
### Act 0 (Hook)
- **Intensity:** Minimal - establish baseline normalcy with faint dread.
- **Grammar:** Subtle frame lag on title card (0.5s desync). Micro-flash with 2 dropped frames + compression smear on cuts.
- **Examples:** Cursor typing with minor warp; title fade with ethereal lens flare (no polish).
- **Consistency:** Cool blues, locked-off shots; artifacts hint at upcoming fracture without distracting.

### Act I (Omen)
- **Intensity:** Subtle - introduce anomalies as omens.
- **Grammar:** Minor smudges (VHS tracking lines on notes), audio-visual desync (subtitle lag ~0.5s on whispers). Shallow DoF with rack focus glitches (e.g., horizon blurs unnaturally).
- **Examples:** Flashback yellow haze with frame drop; raven reflection multiplicity faint.
- **Consistency:** Expansive shadows stretch; artifacts tied to motifs (notes cast long dream-shadows with tear).

### Act II (Recognition)
- **Intensity:** Noticeable - artifacts mirror emotional intimacy and rising cost.
- **Grammar:** Frame wobble on handheld (creeping dread), texture tears in flashbacks (grain explosion on piano motif). Desat to void-grey with temporal blips (e.g., replay trace with echo).
- **Examples:** Speaker ripple with phase-shift; note reading with desync overlap.
- **Consistency:** Warm intrusions expand; artifacts escalate on witness vows (e.g., screen static on "i include you").

### Act III (Catastrophe)
- **Intensity:** Medium-break - full fracture, representing erasure.
- **Grammar:** Frame drops on memory loss, heavy compression artifacts during cutover (audio over black wipes). Strobe warp on rebuild (film burns, desync peaks).
- **Examples:** Sustained piano motif with unresolved fade; flashback merge with present sobs via glitch overlay.
- **Consistency:** One clean shot for contrast (e.g., final note save); otherwise, artifacts dominate to evoke hostile void.

## Generation Notes
- Apply via ComfyUI/FLUX: Include artifact descriptors in prompts (e.g., "VHS grain warp, subtle frame lag").
- QA: Ensure artifacts feel intentional, not erroneous. Tie to scene emotion (e.g., more tear on loss).
- Exception: Preserve one near-perfect clean shot per act for emotional punctuation.
