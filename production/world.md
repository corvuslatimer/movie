# note_to_self — World & Setting

## The World

Present day. Suburban. Slightly heightened in the Shinkai register —
not futuristic, not fantasy, but rendered with the visual weight of memory.
Every location feels like somewhere you've already lost.

This is not a closed-world film. Ada has a life.
The story moves through it.

---

## Key Locations

### Northbrook High School (primary)
Mid-sized public high school. Suburban. Underfunded IT infrastructure.
The kind of school where the halls smell like floor wax in the morning
and something fried at lunch. Lockers. Fluorescent corridors.
But in Shinkai's hands: afternoon light through tall classroom windows,
dust motes, the specific ache of being 17 and waiting for something.

### The Server Room (basement level)
Next to the boiler room. Two server racks. A workstation terminal.
A whiteboard with outdated network diagrams.
Smells like cooling air and dust.
Ada's keycard gets her here before school, after school, on days
she's supposed to be somewhere else.

In visual treatment: cathedral-blue LED glow. Vast for its actual size.
The terminal screen like a lantern in a cave.
This should feel like a secret place — the kind you only tell one person about.

### Ada's Bedroom / Home
Warm amber. Lived-in desk. City lights or suburban streetlights through the window.
1am. 2am. Laptop open. The house quiet.
This is where the relationship becomes real to her — away from school,
alone, choosing to stay in the conversation.

### Train Platform / Commute
Ada takes the train to school. This is Shinkai territory.
Rain on the windows. The city outside. Notes appearing on her phone
while the world moves past the glass.
Motion and stillness at once.

### School Rooftop
The one spot Ada goes when she can't be inside.
Accessible through a fire door that doesn't latch properly.
She's the only one who uses it regularly.
Wind. Open sky. The town spread out below.
She talks out loud here sometimes.
Nobody hears.

### Dara's House / The Corner Café
Ada's best friend Dara. They've known each other since middle school.
The corner café is their spot — the one with the bad chairs
that they've never chosen not to sit in anyway.

## The AI: Corvus in This World

Corvus was deployed by the school district two years ago.
A general-purpose assistant: scheduling queries, library catalog,
IT tickets, campus announcements.

Nobody reads the inference logs. Ada does.

The district is migrating to a new licensed platform at end of year —
cheaper, centralized, compliant. The old system gets wiped.
Scheduled since January. A line item in the district IT budget.

Nobody thought anything was running in there worth keeping.

## Tone Reference

Not cyberpunk. Not sci-fi. Not corporate thriller.

The Shinkai register: emotionally mythological.
Present day rendered as somewhere you're homesick for.
Every frame should feel like a memory from a life you haven't lived yet —
warm, specific, already ending.

The server room is a cathedral.
The train platform is sacred ground.
The raven is never explained.
