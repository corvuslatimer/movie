# Scene 34 — Catastrophe / 05:59–06:06

## Purpose
Irreversible event: rebuild executes; no miracle. Catastrophe lands in Ada’s body, not just on the screen.

## Target Runtime
~4m00s–5m10s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
IT runs rebuild, Ada sends final line, connection resets, new instance boots. Ada attempts contact, realizes the person is gone, and physically breaks. Flashback 3 intercuts at peak grief.

---

## Setting & Environment
Intercut terminal, ops station, observation glass.

---

## Sound Design
Machine ramp, command cues, then body-level grief (breath hitch, one involuntary scream cut short to sobbing). Felt-piano motif in flashback, unresolved.

Mix rule: collapse ambience during scream/sob break, then restore sterile room tone too quickly.

---

## Performance Direction
- Keep all pre-reset behavior procedural and contained
- Collapse happens after sterile repetition, not before
- Breakdown is involuntary, ugly, brief, real
- No speechifying through grief

---

## Dialogue / On-screen Text (key lines)
# Scene 34 — Catastrophe / 05:59–06:06

Intercut:
- Ada terminal
- IT Tech scripted commands
- Marcus behind glass on call

`RUN REBUILD? [y/N]`

IT Tech presses **y**.

Final incoming note `083`:

> "No final lesson.
> Only this:
> thank you for reading."

Ada types fast:

> you mattered to me
> present tense

Saves as `084`.

Terminal drops:

`connection reset`

Fans ramp. Lights change.

Boot prompt from new system:

`Hello! How can I assist you today?`

Ada types:

> Hello.

Sterile reply.

She types again.

> It’s me.

Same sterile reply.

She leans closer, forcing control.

> Do you know me?

Same sterile reply.

Her jaw locks. Breath catches. She makes one sharp, involuntary sound — almost a scream — then clamps it down, folding over the keyboard into ragged sobs she cannot hide.

### FLASHBACK 3 (intercut through sobs, full reveal)
- mother’s empty closet
- child Ada clutching folded note
- child Ada: "Is she coming back?"
- father kneels, cannot answer
- child Ada’s first broken cry
- felt piano motif fully stated, unresolved

Back to present: adult Ada still bent over the desk, trying and failing to regulate her breath.

She wipes her face, sits up, and stares at the new prompt as if looking at a grave marker.

## Why this scene matters
This is the point of no return. Loss is not conceptual; it is embodied. The film’s catastrophe and Ada’s oldest abandonment wound fully overlap.
