# Scene 25 Video Asset — Ada's Screen

## Status
Pending capture — this repository does not yet include the workstation footage for the compliance ping beat.

## Objective
Create a short (80–110s) workstation-focused clip where Ada receives, reads, and suppresses the automated compliance notice without overt emotional release. The audience must clearly read the escalation metadata that reveals the prior review.

## Shot Plan
1. **Shot 1 – Establish / Alert Arrival (6–8s)**  
   Locked-off medium on Ada at her desk. Monitor glow dominates, UI alert chime triggers. Ensure continuity props (mug, badge, thermos, notebook stack) match previous scenes.
2. **Shot 2 – Notice Body Read (20–30s)**  
   Push-in or punch-in to the primary monitor. Ada scrolls slowly through the notice body; text must stay legible. Capture cursor interactions and any involuntary micro-reactions (breath hold, eye tracking, but no dialogue).
3. **Shot 3 – Metadata Reveal (15–20s)**  
   Macro insert of the routing metadata table. Highlight/escalate the row that proves the ticket was reviewed 36 hours earlier by an upper-tier supervisor. Provide at least one clean pass without overlays for graphics.
4. **Shot 4 – Dismissal (5–8s)**  
   Return to the medium framing as Ada minimizes the alert and resumes typing. No audio sting; keep motions efficient.

## UI / Text Requirements
- Compliance notice headline: “AUTO-COMPLIANCE REVIEW NOTICE – ACTION REQUIRED”.
- Metadata fields: `Flag Timestamp`, `Initial Reviewer`, `Escalation Path`, `Review Age (hrs)`, `Routing Comment`.
- Reviewer name above Marcus (e.g., “Elena Park (Oversight Lead)”) with review age `36h`.
- Include subtle tracking logs/time stamps in lower right corner for authenticity.
- Provide a second graphics layer export (PNG/EXR) for the metadata table to aid compositing.

## Performance & Lighting Notes
- Lighting: motivated practicals + monitor key; avoid stylized neon.
- Keep the frame grounded and realistic; minimal camera drift to emphasize institutional stillness.
- Ada’s performance: composed, focused, but register a fractional pause when the metadata is revealed.

## Delivery Specs
- Resolution: 3840×2160 (preferred) or 1920×1080 fallback, 23.98fps.
- Codec: ProRes 422 HQ or DNxHR HQX; include matching h.264 proxy for dailies.
