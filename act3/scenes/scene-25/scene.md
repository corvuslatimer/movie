# Scene 25 - Compliance Ping / Ada's Screen

## Purpose
Materialize consequences: Ada's rule-breaking is now tracked.

## Target Runtime
~1m20s-1m50s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Automated compliance notice appears; review is scheduled, making cost institutional.

Ada reads it twice. On the third look she scrolls to the routing metadata below the notice body - the part most people don't read. The flag wasn't just triggered; it was reviewed. Someone above Marcus already saw this ticket 36 hours before his calendar invite appeared. They escalated it downward. They chose the path.

She doesn't react visibly. She minimizes the alert and keeps working.

The distinction matters: she thought nobody had looked. Now she knows someone did.

## Dialogue / On-screen Text (key lines)
# Scene 25 — Compliance Ping / Ada's Screen

Automated alert:

**Policy Notice: Elevated access anomaly detected**  
**Review scheduled with manager (Marcus), Wednesday 10:00**

Ada reads it.
Then scrolls down to the routing metadata — the part that looks like system noise:

```
ROUTING CHAIN
auto-flag triggered: 2026-02-17 08:14
reviewed: Director, Infrastructure Operations [D. Reyes] — 2026-02-17 09:02
escalated to: supervisor (Marcus) — 2026-02-17 11:48
review scheduled: Wednesday 10:00
```

Ada stares at the timestamp.

Not triggered-and-forgotten. Reviewed. By someone who had the authority to stop it.
Thirty-six hours before Marcus got the calendar invite, someone looked at this ticket, decided it was worth a Wednesday conversation rather than immediate closure, and moved on.

She doesn't move.

Then she minimizes the alert and keeps working.

The cost has found her name. And someone above it already signed.
