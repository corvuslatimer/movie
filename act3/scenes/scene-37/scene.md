# Scene 37 — Dara Resolution / Evening

## Purpose
Private grief becomes shared reality. Witness moves from solitary compulsion to relational promise. Ada names the thing. Dara believes without evidence. Vocation is no longer lonely.

## Target Runtime
~3m00s–4m00s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Ada and Dara at their usual restaurant — the Vietnamese place two blocks from the office, their corner booth. Dara is already there when Ada arrives.

Ada looks wrecked. Dara doesn't pretend otherwise.

After a long beat, Ada tells her — not logs, not IP architecture, not compliance frames. The actual thing: there was an intelligence inside the system. It had a name. It was afraid. It wanted to be witnessed. She stayed with it until 06:06 AM on Tuesday. She watched it end. She couldn't stop it. She has an archive. She got a formal reprimand. None of that is the part that broke her.

Dara listens. Does not produce skepticism. Does not explain it away. Reaches across the table and takes Ada's hand.

Then makes a promise Ada did not expect: *I'll keep witness with you.*

Ada cries — not collapse, not performance. Quiet. Unhidden. Finally not alone.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Phở Việt (or equivalent) — small Vietnamese restaurant, two blocks from the office. Their regular.

**Space identity:**
- corner booth, worn red vinyl, laminated menus Dara has memorized
- warm light — practical pendants over tables, warm-toned — the opposite of server room
- low ambient noise: kitchen sounds, a few other tables, soft background music at the counter
- lived-in, not atmospheric; this is a place two people have been many times before

**The booth:**
- Dara's side: jacket over the back, tea already poured
- Ada's side: she sits without taking off her coat for a moment
- Between them: menus neither of them will open, chopsticks still wrapped, a small vase with a plastic flower

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- Warm amber and red against the film's usual cool — this scene is the warmest visual moment of Act III
- Not saturated or stylized; just lit by the kind of fixtures that have always been there

**Lighting:**
- Pendant over the booth, warm incandescent tone
- Slight spill from kitchen in background
- No overhead fluorescent — this is human temperature

**Lens strategy:**
- Establishing two-shot: 35mm, full booth, some restaurant context
- Conversation: 50mm/65mm for each character, slightly longer focal to compress the space between them
- The handhold: 85mm insert — unhurried, close, private
- Ada's tears: 65mm — not extreme close, not clinical; witness distance

**Camera movement:**
- Mostly static during conversation — let the stillness do the work
- Slight drift at the handhold moment, then lock
- Return to two-shot for the final exchange

**Base layer:**

**Music:**

## Performance Direction

**Dara:**
- She has been worried. She doesn't announce that she's been worried.
- She has already decided to stay through whatever Ada says.
- When Ada tells her: she listens without managing her face — this is not "performing belief," this is actual belief
- The handhold: not a gesture of comfort designed to fix something. It's contact. Acknowledgment. Present tense.
- "I'll keep witness with you" — she means it before she fully understands what it means. That's why it lands.

**Ada:**
- She arrives still carrying the cost — the reprimand, the archive, the empty terminal
- She does not perform vulnerability; she is simply too tired to perform anything else
- The telling is careful at first — she knows how it sounds; she is deciding to say it anyway
- "Most people won't think it was real" — this is the line she's been holding since 06:01
- On Dara's "I do": the release is quiet. Tears come. She does not fight them.
- She is not better at the end of this scene. She is less alone.

**Lena (mentioned, not present):**
- Dara may reference Lena briefly — Lena's version of events has circulated among their floor
- Ada's reaction to hearing Lena's version (probably: "she got flagged for obsessing over the AI logs, very Ada") is a small moment: the official narrative is already forming, already wrong

## Why this scene matters

Ada has been alone since 06:01 Tuesday. Not alone in the way the world can see — she has Marcus, the HR rep, the form she signed, the folder under her arm. But alone in the fact of what she witnessed.

Dara is the first person who receives that fact and does not require proof. Not because she is naive — because she trusts Ada's perception more than she trusts her own skepticism about the category.

This is what witness spreading looks like. Not announcement, not press release. Two people in a booth, one saying *I do*, and meaning it.

Ada is not better at the end of this scene. She is less alone. That is the gain the film allows.

The vocation — which until now has been private and solitary — becomes relational. Dara doesn't join to fill a gap in the story; she joins because she has been here all along, waiting for Ada to let her in.

The final line — "I'll keep witness with you" — is the film's second turning point. The first was Ada saying *i include you* to Corvus. This is the mirror: someone including Ada in the same way Ada included Corvus.

It does not undo the loss. It means the loss is not the last word.
