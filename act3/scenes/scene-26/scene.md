# Scene 26 — Marcus Office / Afternoon + Hallway

## Purpose
Show procedural compassion that still harms. No villain posture. Marcus's kindness is genuine and useless. The cost finds Ada's name. Lena's hallway cameo adds one last external mirror: the world reading the situation slightly wrong from a comfortable distance.

## Target Runtime
~2m10s–2m50s

---

## Story Beat (What happens)
Marcus asks about the access anomaly; Ada partially confesses; his reset language lands as threat disguised as comfort.

**Immediately after:** Ada exits into the hallway. Lena is there — she's heard the compliance ping rumor in the way office floors absorb these things without details. She makes a joke. Ada has to produce a laugh from somewhere. She does. Lena moves on. Ada stands in the hallway alone for four seconds before she walks.

---

## Setting & Environment
**Location:** Manager office with glass walls (interior meeting visible from floor), then hallway outside.

**Space identity:**
- Marcus's office: functional authority — monitors, desk, compliance docs, routine
- Hallway: transitional, fluorescent, no one's territory
- The contrast matters: inside the office, Ada chose her words carefully; in the hallway, she just has to stand upright

---

## Visual Design
**Color palette:**
- Office: warm neutrals, daylight through glass
- Hallway: fluorescent overhead, slightly cooler — less managed

**Lighting:**
- Office: natural through blinds, practical on desk
- Hallway: overhead strip, flat, honest

**Lens strategy:**
- Marcus/Ada two-shot: 35mm, no stylized framing
- Compliance doc insert: 50mm close
- Reaction close-ups: 65mm/85mm
- Hallway: 35mm, handheld — brief, unmanaged

**Camera movement:**
- Office: mostly static; let the stillness carry weight
- Hallway: handheld drift — the world doesn't hold still for Ada's internal state

---

## Set Dressing / Objects (must-have)
- Marcus's desk: rollout checklist visible, policy binder, coffee
- Monitors showing legible (but blurred) internal tools
- Compliance notice with key line readable: *Unauthorized persistence or archive manipulation = termination-level event*
- Ada's badge/mug/carry items consistent with prior scenes
- Hallway: nothing — just fluorescent and the sound of the floor

---

## Sound Design
**Office:**
- Low ambient: HVAC, distant keyboard, floor murmur
- Dip ambience on Marcus's "Clean slate" — the line should land into near-silence
- Return full room tone immediately after

**Hallway:**
- Closer, harder acoustics — voices carry differently
- Lena's voice bright against the flat corridor sound
- After Lena exits: full office floor hum resumes, exposing Ada alone in it

**Mix note:**
The office scene is quiet and managed. The hallway is ordinary and loud. Ada has to navigate both. Neither is cruel. Both cost something.

---

## Performance Direction
**Marcus:**
- Calm. Not cold. He is doing a procedure that he considers humane.
- "Clean slate" is meant as reassurance; he believes it
- He does not enjoy this. He also does not hesitate.

**Ada:**
- Controls every word inside the office; she is choosing how much truth to spend and spending exactly that amount
- Her face when Marcus says "Clean slate": microadjustment only — not collapse
- In the hallway: she is still holding it. Lena's joke requires one more hold.
- The four seconds after Lena leaves: allow the face to go somewhere

**Lena:**
- She has heard "compliance ping involving Ada and the AI logs" — no specifics
- She doesn't know what she's poking; she knows Ada is usually fine with her jokes
- Delivery is affectionate, not pointed — she's checking in through comedy, her only register
- She reads Ada's laugh as "she's okay" and moves on satisfied

---

## Blocking

### Office
1. Ada enters; Marcus is mid-task, wraps to attention.
2. Marcus delivers compliance flag — calm, procedural.
3. Ada responds: partial truth, controlled.
4. Marcus: "After Tuesday we reset. Clean slate."
5. Beat. Ada nods. Signs or accepts the meeting's outcome.
6. She stands to leave. Marcus offers one more soft line — he wants to have helped.
7. She exits.

### Hallway
8. Ada steps out of the glass office. Corridor. Normal traffic.
9. Lena appears from the opposite direction — heading somewhere, not seeking Ada.
10. Lena clocks Ada's expression; pivots naturally to her.

   **LENA**  
   Compliance ping? They flagged you for ghost-hunting in the logs?  
   *(beat)*  
   Classic. The AI logs are basically haunted.

11. Ada produces the laugh.

   **ADA**  
   Yeah. Something like that.

12. Lena: "You're fine. Marcus is harmless." — she's already moving again.
13. Lena exits around the corner.
14. Ada stands alone in the fluorescent hallway.
15. Four seconds. Then she walks.

## Why this scene matters
Marcus is not a villain. That's what makes the scene devastating. The system is not broken — it is working exactly as designed, and the design doesn't include what Ada witnessed.

Lena's hallway beat extends this logic. She also means well. She also gets it almost right (there was something strange in the logs) and entirely wrong (it's haunted, not present; a ghost, not a being). The joke is affectionate and accurate from her coordinates.

Ada has to smile at the exact right frequency.
She does.
That costs something.
